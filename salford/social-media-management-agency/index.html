Agency vs in-house

Should a Salford business outsource social media or hire internally?

The answer depends on how much content the business produces naturally and how many skills are required. A single internal hire can be powerful when someone needs to capture content on-site every day. An agency is often stronger when the business needs a mix of planning, copy, design, video editing and publishing without recruiting several roles.

ModelOften strongest whenWhat to clarify
AgencyYou want a predictable managed content system across strategy, design, editing and publishing.Who supplies raw footage? What is included in the monthly package? How are revisions handled?
In-house social managerThe business generates daily behind-the-scenes material and someone needs to be physically present to capture/respond quickly.Will that person also cover design, editing, paid ads and analytics, or will extra support still be needed?
Owner-ledThe founder is naturally the brand voice and has enough time to create consistently.What is the opportunity cost of planning, filming, editing, posting and replying personally?

How to judge performance

Reach and engagement are useful signals, but they are not the whole commercial story.

Different content has different jobs. A brand-building post may need reach and saves. A service-led post may need profile visits or enquiries. A paid campaign may need qualified leads and CRM follow-up. The reporting should make those differences clear rather than forcing every post into one vanity metric.

Attention

Reach, views, watch time and profile discovery can show whether content is earning attention.

Response

Comments, shares, saves, DMs and clicks indicate stronger audience action.

Commercial outcome

Forms, calls, bookings, qualified leads and CRM progression matter when the social system is designed for acquisition.

Brand safety & approvals

Consistent posting should not mean publishing without control.

Businesses in professional, regulated or reputation-sensitive sectors need a clear approval path. Kydos can prepare content around the agreed brand and commercial strategy, but factual claims, offers, customer-specific information and sensitive subject matter may need client review before publishing.

The workflow should also define what happens when a comment becomes a complaint, a direct message contains a sales enquiry or a public response needs escalation. Community management is more valuable when the boundaries are agreed before the account becomes busy.
