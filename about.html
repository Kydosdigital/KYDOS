Our story

An agency that grew by solving the next problem.

Kydos Digital Limited was incorporated in Manchester in 2025. The early work was practical: websites, social content, campaigns and hands-on digital support. As the work developed, the same pattern kept appearing. A campaign could generate attention, but the website still needed to convert it. A form could capture a lead, but somebody still had to route and follow it up. Reporting could show performance, but only if the tracking was trustworthy. Kydos grew by connecting those pieces.

2025

Hands-on digital delivery

Client work centred on social media, content, websites, SEO and campaign support for growing businesses and community organisations.

2025–26

From channels to acquisition systems

Projects expanded into paid media, conversion tracking, lead qualification, CRM workflows, email sequences, event funnels and structured reporting.

2026

Marketing meets software and automation

Kydos work now includes connected marketing systems and digital products, from GoHighLevel automations and lead-routing logic to AI-enabled dashboards and API-based applications.

Today

One team around the outcome

We still sell individual services when that is all a business needs. The difference is that we can see how the website, content, search, paid media, CRM, automation and reporting fit together before recommending more activity.

How we work

Useful before impressive.

Our best work starts with a clear commercial objective, an honest view of the current system and a practical definition of what success will look like. That keeps strategy connected to implementation and stops digital activity becoming a collection of disconnected tasks.

Clarity before complexity

We define the problem, audience, conversion and measurement before adding channels or software.

Evidence over guesswork

Claims, budgets, tracking and recommendations should be grounded in real data wherever it is available.

Implementation matters

A strategy is only useful when somebody owns the landing page, tracking, creative, CRM rule or technical fix that makes it real.

Clients should understand what they own

Websites, advertising accounts, analytics, creative assets and data should not become mysterious black boxes.
