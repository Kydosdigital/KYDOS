Tameside geography

East Manchester overlap should not make paid targeting vague.

A business may serve the whole borough, one centre such as Ashton-under-Lyne, a corridor through Hyde and Denton, or customers across Tameside and Manchester. The campaign should reflect fulfilment reality rather than automatically targeting every postcode in Greater Manchester.

If lead quality differs by area, geography can become part of optimisation. If it does not, splitting the account into many small local campaigns can reduce learning and add unnecessary complexity.

Conversion tracking

Clicks are not the KPI if the business needs leads.

Primary conversions should represent meaningful actions such as a qualified form submission, phone call, booking or purchase. CTA clicks and form starts can be useful diagnostic signals, but they should not inflate lead reporting.

SignalHow to treat it
Qualified form submissionPrimary conversion when it reflects a genuine enquiry.
Phone callPrimary conversion when call quality can be reviewed.
CTA click or form startUseful supporting signal, not automatically a lead.
Page viewDiagnostic behaviour, not a conversion by itself.
