Tameside website architecture

Local pages should exist only when they improve the customer journey.

A business serving Tameside may need one strong borough page, service pages with local proof, or carefully selected town pages where demand is distinct. It does not automatically need separate near-identical pages for Ashton-under-Lyne, Hyde, Denton, Stalybridge and Droylsden.

Because Droylsden and parts of western Tameside overlap strongly with Manchester buying behaviour, page ownership matters. Search intent, real fulfilment and customer evidence should decide where localisation stops.
