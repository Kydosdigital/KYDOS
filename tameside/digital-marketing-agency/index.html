The short answer

What should a digital marketing agency do for a Tameside business?

It should identify the highest-value commercial problem, choose the smallest useful channel mix, make implementation responsibilities clear, and measure whether the work creates the action the business actually needs. More activity is not automatically a better strategy.

Tameside local strategy

Local marketing should follow the real customer footprint.

Tameside businesses may serve one town, several borough centres, east Manchester or a wider Greater Manchester catchment. Organic pages and paid campaigns should reflect fulfilment reality rather than automatically treating every nearby place as a separate target.

Ashton-under-Lyne is the borough's principal commercial centre, while Hyde, Denton, Stalybridge and Droylsden create different local catchments and different levels of Manchester overlap. The right structure depends on actual customers, search demand and whether locality changes the offer.

Buyer decision

Compare responsibility, not just a list of channels.

Before choosing a provider, confirm who owns strategy, content, development, account access, tracking, approvals and lead follow-up. Two proposals can contain the same channel names while leaving very different amounts of work with your team.

What Kydos controls

The agreed delivery scope, implementation that access permits, reporting and optimisation.

What the client controls

Business facts, approvals, access, operational capacity and sales follow-up unless otherwise scoped.

What remains separate

Advertising spend and any third-party costs should be visible rather than hidden inside a vague retainer.
