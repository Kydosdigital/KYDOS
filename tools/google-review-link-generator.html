How a direct Google review link works

Every business on Google Maps has a unique Place ID. Google provides a special address that opens the review box for that business straight away:

https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID

Replace YOUR_PLACE_ID with your own Place ID and you have a link that takes customers directly to the star rating, with no searching. The generator above does this for you and turns the link into a QR code.

How to get more Google reviews

  1. Ask at the right moment. Straight after a job well done or a compliment is when people are most willing.
  2. Remove the friction. A direct link skips searching and clicking, which is where most people give up.
  3. Reply to every review. Good or bad, a short personal reply shows future customers you care.
  4. Never buy or incentivise reviews. It breaks Google's rules and can get reviews removed.
