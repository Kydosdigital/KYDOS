Tips that keep your data tidy

  • Always use lowercase. Analytics treats "Facebook" and "facebook" as two different sources. This tool lowercases for you.
  • Track your Google Business Profile website link. Otherwise those visits get lumped in with ordinary Google searches.
  • Never add UTMs to links between pages on your own site. It resets the visitor's original source.

Frequently asked questions

What is a UTM link?
A normal web address with tracking tags added to the end, such as utm_source and utm_medium. Google Analytics reads them to show where each visitor came from. For example: https://www.yourbusiness.co.uk/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=google-business-profile

Which UTM parameters do I need?
Source and medium are the essentials. Campaign is recommended for promotions, and content helps tell two links in the same post apart.

Should I use UTMs on my Google Business Profile?
Yes. Otherwise those visits get mixed in with ordinary Google search traffic.

Is this UTM builder free?
Yes. No sign-up, and nothing you enter is stored; it all runs in your browser.
