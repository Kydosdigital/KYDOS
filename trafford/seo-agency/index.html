The short answer

What should an SEO agency do for a Trafford business?

It should understand what people search for, assign each important commercial intent to the strongest existing or planned page, fix technical barriers, improve the usefulness of those pages and build supporting content only where it adds a distinct answer. Local SEO should reflect genuine service areas rather than manufacture town pages for every possible modifier.

Trafford search architecture

Altrincham, Sale and Trafford Park can represent different search problems.

Altrincham and Sale can generate strong local consumer and professional-service intent, while Stretford and Urmston may matter for service-radius and neighbourhood discovery. Trafford Park introduces a different commercial pattern, where B2B buyers may care more about capability, sector expertise, logistics and regional reach than a local office claim.

The answer is not a page for every place and service combination. A strong Trafford service page can own borough-level intent, Manchester pages can own broader city searches, and town pages should only be added after demand and distinctness are validated. This reduces cannibalisation and makes internal links clearer for both users and search engines.
