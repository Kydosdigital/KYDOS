The short answer

What should a PPC agency do for a Trafford business?

It should control search intent, location, budget, landing-page relevance and conversion tracking as one system. The objective is not to maximise clicks. It is to spend against useful demand, remove irrelevant queries and areas, and improve the journey that turns paid visits into qualified actions.

Trafford paid geography

Altrincham, Sale and Trafford Park should not automatically share one targeting rule.

A trades or appointment-led business may only want leads within a practical radius around Sale, Stretford or Urmston. An Altrincham professional service may draw clients from Trafford, south Manchester and Cheshire. A Trafford Park B2B company may serve the North West or national market even though its physical base sits inside Trafford.

The campaign should reflect those economics. Tight geography can protect budget for local services, but over-restricting a regional B2B campaign can remove valuable demand. Location reporting should be reviewed alongside actual lead quality rather than treated as a one-time setup task.

PPC and SEO

Use paid search learning without creating duplicate organic pages.

Paid search can reveal which queries and messages generate qualified action quickly. That data can inform SEO and landing-page priorities, but it should not trigger a new organic page for every campaign variation. The organic architecture still needs one strongest URL per distinct intent.
