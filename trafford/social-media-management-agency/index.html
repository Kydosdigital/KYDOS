Trafford audience fit

A borough-wide feed still needs audience-specific decisions.

Altrincham and Sale can support premium consumer, property, hospitality and professional-service audiences where visual quality, trust and local familiarity matter. Stretford and Urmston may place more weight on practical local relevance, service proof and community connection. Trafford Park can require a more commercial B2B voice, with expertise, capability and decision-maker distribution carrying more weight than lifestyle content.

This does not mean creating separate social accounts for every town. It means understanding which audience segments exist, choosing the right platform and content mix, and avoiding generic local hashtags as a substitute for useful communication.

Organic and paid

Do not confuse content management with advertising spend.

Organic social management creates and manages the ongoing content presence. Paid social advertising is a separate media activity with its own campaign setup, budget, targeting, conversion tracking and optimisation requirements. A proposal should state whether paid advertising management is included, and media spend should be separated from the agency fee.
