The Lifeline Company · Content strategy

Building a high-frequency content system without turning therapy into generic social media advice.

The Lifeline Company required five pieces of content per week across a mix of reels, carousels and image posts. The challenge was to keep that cadence useful, human and evidence-aware while adapting to changing filming availability.

The brief

Useful therapeutic content, not recycled motivational posts.

Content needed to speak to real experiences such as burnout, relationships, working motherhood, mental load and identity while respecting professional boundaries.

Kydos role

Research, content architecture, scripts and production-ready creative direction.

We created the content system, adapted formats around available footage and built recurring educational and conversational series.

01 · Editorial architecture

Specific audiences, recurring formats, genuine insight.

The system was designed to avoid writing every post for "everyone". Content could speak directly to a working mum carrying the mental load, a couple who feel like flatmates, the always-on professional or the person everyone else relies on.

Lifeline Classroom

Educational carousel content used to explain useful psychological and relationship concepts clearly.

Lifeline Conversations

More reflective content built around questions, identity, relationships and the internal experiences people often struggle to name.

Relatable reels

POV, silent and text-overlay video concepts made the content executable even when new talking-head filming was limited.

02 · Evidence & claims

Research first when the content becomes substantive.

Therapy content carries a different responsibility from ordinary brand copy. The content process therefore treats substantive psychological claims differently from light commentary or lived-experience observations.

Evidence-aware topics

Where a post makes a substantive claim, the content is checked against appropriate sources such as NHS, NICE, professional bodies or recognised psychological frameworks.

Careful interpretation

Content distinguishes what a source directly supports from the agency's interpretation, and avoids presenting broad social-media statements as clinical fact.

03 · Production resilience

A content system that could continue when filming changed.

When new filming was not immediately available, the plan shifted towards POV reels, b-roll, text-overlay reels, carousels and static posts rather than allowing the content calendar to stall.

A reliable content system should survive a change in filming schedule without lowering the intellectual quality of the work.
