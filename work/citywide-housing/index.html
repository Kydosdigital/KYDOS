Citywide Housing · SEO, content & CRM

Turning landlord search demand into a structured acquisition system.

Citywide Housing needed more than a publishing schedule. The work connected SEO content, problem-to-solution messaging, lead capture and GoHighLevel routing so organic activity could move landlords from search to follow-up.

The brief

Build reliable organic acquisition around guaranteed rent.

Content needed to answer changing landlord questions, build topical authority and guide suitable prospects towards the guaranteed-rent proposition without relying on paid media.

Kydos role

Strategy, SEO, content, CRM and reporting.

We structured the content funnel, maintained the publishing programme, tracked performance and connected website leads into qualification and routing workflows.

01 · Strategy

Three themes, one acquisition journey.

Instead of treating blogging as a volume exercise, the May programme was organised around three connected themes that reflected different stages of landlord decision-making.

Awareness

Renters Rights Act

Guidance around possession, arrears, repairs, buy-to-let viability and changing landlord obligations attracted people searching for answers.

Problem recognition

Risk & compliance

Content explored turnover costs, regulatory burden, burnout and the operational weight of traditional management.

Solution

Guaranteed rent

Solution-led content connected those pressures to predictable income, reduced void exposure and a more hands-off leasing model.

02 · Delivery

A sustained programme, not a one-off campaign.

The work combined regular publishing with on-page optimisation, reporting and lead handling. That matters because organic acquisition compounds only when the content, conversion route and follow-up system stay connected.

Content & SEO

  • Approximately five SEO-optimised articles per week in May.
  • 22 published articles for the month.
  • Average TruSEO score of 84.6, with one article reaching 100/100.
  • Internal funnel logic from awareness topics towards solution content.

Lead & CRM system

  • Website enquiries captured into GoHighLevel.
  • Lead qualification and routing based on location and property status.
  • Relevant team members notified for follow-up.
  • Warm lead status used for suitable website enquiries.
