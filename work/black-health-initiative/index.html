Black Health Initiative · Social & content

Making community health communications useful, specific and culturally grounded.

Black Health Initiative works around health inequalities affecting Black and Brown communities. Kydos supported its social content with a practical mix of educational posts, community information, counselling promotion and event-led communications.

The brief

Keep social content useful without becoming generic health messaging.

Posts needed to promote services and community initiatives while still sounding relevant to the people BHI serves.

Kydos role

Content planning, copy, creative direction and campaign structure.

We created recurring content calendars and translated changing priorities into static posts, carousels, reels and event campaign content.

01 · Content system

From service promotion to everyday relevance.

The programme covered a wide range of real community concerns rather than treating health awareness as one narrow theme.

Mental health & counselling

Content promoted access to counselling while using language designed to feel supportive rather than clinical or alarmist.

Financial wellbeing

Posts covered money stress, safer borrowing and community protection alongside wider wellbeing messages.

Community health

Content explained BHI's role, community spaces, events and practical support rather than relying only on awareness-day posts.

02 · Formats

A repeatable mix the creative team could actually produce.

Each calendar translated the message into a production-ready format with copy, on-screen text, captions, calls to action and hashtags.
