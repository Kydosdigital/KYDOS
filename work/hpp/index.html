HPP · PPC audit & trade campaign support

Finding the expensive parts of paid media before adding more spend.

For HPP, a trade supplier serving fitters, furniture makers and installers, Kydos combined a Google Ads efficiency review with claim-checked creative support for a product-led social campaign.

The audit

Not every campaign was producing value at the same cost.

The account review found a large cost gap between remarketing and a brand-awareness campaign, alongside competitor-brand spend and missing call-conversion tracking.

The campaign

Creative needed to be accurate as well as persuasive.

For the Glide Frameless campaign, Kydos verified product claims against the supplied landing pages before producing social copy and the designer brief.

01 · Paid media audit

Make the cost gap visible.

The account review did not treat all conversions as equivalent. It compared campaign efficiency, isolated avoidable competitor-brand spend and identified a measurement gap around phone calls.

Efficiency

Remarketing was delivering conversions at a materially lower audited cost than the brand-awareness campaign.

Search terms

Competitor-brand spend and a dead-weight competitor ad group were identified as areas for cleanup.

Tracking

Missing call-conversion tracking was flagged so future reporting could better reflect real enquiry behaviour.

02 · Campaign QA

Check the product facts before the creative leaves the agency.

The Glide Frameless campaign included a claim-by-claim proofing step against the landing-page source material.

Corrections caught

The review identified inaccurate product details around décor count, width and a handle-colour description before they became public-facing campaign claims.

Delivery

Kydos produced eight Facebook and Instagram posts, a proofing document and a detailed designer brief for the trade-focused campaign.

What this project shows

Good campaign management is partly optimisation and partly quality control: spend should be accountable, tracking should be complete and the product claims should be right.
