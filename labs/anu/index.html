ANU · Community marketplace

A two-sided marketplace built around community commerce.

ANU combines food ordering, store and service discovery, bookings, community features and business-side CRM tools inside one platform, with Stripe Connect handling marketplace payment flows.

Consumer side

Discover, order and book.

Users can move across food, stores, services and booking journeys rather than relying on separate platforms for each type of community business.

Business side

Connected-account onboarding and business tools.

The product includes business onboarding, payment flows, webhooks and dashboard/CRM functionality for participating businesses.

Marketplace

Food, retail, services and bookings in one community product.

Payments

Stripe Connect split-payment and connected-account workflows.

Business CRM

Operational tools and dashboard context for marketplace businesses.

Stack

Built as a full application, not a directory theme.

The documented stack includes React, TypeScript, Supabase with Postgres, row-level security, Edge Functions and Storage, Stripe Connect, Resend and selected AI integrations.

The hard part of a marketplace is not listing businesses. It is coordinating identity, inventory or availability, payment state and the workflows on both sides.

Manchester-based digital agency connecting strategy, creative, search, paid media, websites, CRM, automation and reporting.
