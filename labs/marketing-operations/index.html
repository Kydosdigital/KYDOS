Kydos Labs · Marketing operations

The systems that sit between a lead and the work that follows.

Kydos has developed internal CRM and workflow automation around lead capture, qualification, routing, nurture, appointment handling and reporting. This is the operating layer behind many of the client journeys we design.

Why it exists

Marketing creates work for operations.

A form submission is not useful if nobody owns the follow-up, the source is lost or the contact sits unqualified in a generic inbox.

What we build

Structured states and automated next actions.

Workflows can create or update CRM records, tag source and intent, route leads, schedule follow-up and surface exceptions for human review.

Lead capture

Forms, webhooks and source data enter one structured record.

Qualification & routing

Rules and data points determine ownership and next action.

Nurture

Email, SMS or task sequences support longer decision journeys.

Appointments

Confirmation, reminder, no-show and follow-up logic reduce manual administration.

Reporting

Pipeline states and conversion events create a clearer view of what happened after the lead.

Human gates

High-risk, ambiguous or commercially important actions can stay approval-led rather than fully automatic.

Tooling

Workflow first, tools second.

Documented Kydos automation work spans GoHighLevel / LeadConnector, Make, n8n, Airtable, Clay, webhooks and custom API integrations. The tool changes by use case, but the lifecycle model should remain understandable.
