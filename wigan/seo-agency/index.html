Wigan vs Manchester search intent

Wigan's local demand can overlap with wider Greater Manchester intent, creating both opportunity and cannibalisation risk.

A Wigan company may serve Leigh, Standish, Hindley, Atherton, Wigan town centre and Manchester customers. That does not mean the website needs separate pages for every wording variation. The SEO architecture should decide which page owns each commercial intent and which local references genuinely improve the answer.

For example, “SEO agency Wigan” and “SEO company Wigan” are normally better consolidated into one excellent commercial page than split between two competing URLs. Pricing, near-me and shortlist queries can support that page when they answer different buyer questions.

Google Business Profile & local search

Local SEO is more than adding “Wigan” to page titles.

For eligible local businesses, Google Business Profile can be an important part of the search journey because buyers may compare map results, reviews, opening information and proximity before visiting a website. But the profile and the website should reinforce each other rather than operate as two unrelated assets.

Local SEO areaWhat good execution looks like
Business informationName, address/service area, contact details and categories are accurate and consistent with the real business.
Service relevanceThe website clearly explains the services the profile is expected to support instead of relying on the profile alone.
Location pagesPages are created only when they answer a distinct local buying need, not because every nearby place name can be turned into a URL.
Reviews and proofGenuine customer evidence is used where approved; no invented local testimonials or ratings.
MeasurementSearch Console, analytics and conversion tracking are used to understand whether local visibility is creating useful website actions.

Content clusters

Supporting blogs should strengthen the money page, not float around the site.

A useful Wigan SEO cluster might contain one commercial landing page for the main service intent, then distinct supporting articles around prices, near-me selection, contracts, agency comparisons and package questions. Those supporting pages should link back to the commercial target and to each other when the relationship is genuinely helpful.

This gives search engines a clearer idea of which URL is the primary answer and gives users several ways to enter the same buying journey without creating five pages that all compete for “SEO agency Wigan”.

The practical test

Can you explain why every page exists?

If two URLs answer the same question with slightly different wording, the safer strategy is normally to consolidate. If they answer different decisions, such as “how much does it cost?” versus “which provider should I shortlist?”, the pages can support one another.

Technical SEO audit

What should a technical SEO review actually look for?

A useful audit should identify issues that can materially affect crawling, indexing, page understanding or user experience, then rank them by business impact. A long export of every possible warning is less useful than a short list of problems that block important commercial pages.

AreaWhy it matters
Crawlability & robotsImportant pages need to be accessible to search engines while low-value or private areas should not waste crawl attention.
Indexation & canonicalsThe preferred URL should be clear, and duplicate or accidental noindex states should be identified.
Internal linkingCommercial pages need enough contextual links from hubs and supporting content to be discovered and understood.
Redirects & broken linksMigration mistakes and dead links can waste authority and create poor user journeys.
Structured dataSchema should match visible content and accurately describe the organisation, service, breadcrumbs and FAQs where relevant.
Performance & mobile UXTechnical performance should support real users and not undermine conversion on the devices customers actually use.
