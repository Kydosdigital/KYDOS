How we scope

The smallest sensible solution first.

A business with a conversion problem may not need more traffic. A business with strong demand but slow follow-up may need CRM before more ads. The scope should follow the bottleneck.

Diagnose

Understand the commercial goal and the current journey.

Prioritise

Identify the constraint most likely to change the outcome.

Deliver

Build the service, page, campaign or workflow required.

Measure

Use performance and sales feedback to decide what comes next.
