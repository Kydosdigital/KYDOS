## Pilot A: Why isn't my business showing on Google?

**Canonical article:** https://www.kydosdigital.com/blog/why-business-not-showing-google.html

### A1: Instagram/Facebook carousel, 6 slides
**Audience:** Owner of a local service business in Oldham, Stockport or Manchester who cannot find their business in Google Search or Maps.

1. **Cover:** “Your business isn't showing on Google. What should you check first?”
2. **Different problems:** “Is your website missing from Search, or is your Google Business Profile missing from Maps? They need different checks.”
3. **Website check:** “Use Google Search Console URL Inspection to check whether the page is indexed. Indexing is not the same as ranking.”
4. **Maps check:** “Check your Business Profile eligibility, verification and actual customer-facing/service-area details. A virtual office is not automatically eligible.”
5. **Competitor comparison:** “Local ranking considers relevance, distance and prominence. A verified profile is not a promise of a top Maps position.”
6. **Action:** “Use our free nine-check worksheet before paying for SEO. Find the full guide on Kydos Digital's blog.”

**Caption draft:** “Not showing on Google doesn't always mean you need to buy SEO. First, find out whether the issue is your website, your Business Profile or your visibility for a specific service. Our nine-check guide shows where to start. Read it on the Kydos blog. #SmallBusinessUK #LocalSEO #GreaterManchester #GoogleBusinessProfile #BusinessGrowth”

**Design direction:** Neutral branded typography, simple browser/search and Maps icons, high-contrast large text. Do not use fake Google screenshots or fabricated local rankings. The term Google Business Profile should not imply affiliation with Google.

**Evidence and checks:** https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091 and https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177 . Confirm current official rules before posting.

### A2: 35-45 second talking-head/animated-tip video
**Hook (0-5s):** “You searched for your business and couldn't find it. Don't pay for SEO until you've checked this.”
**5-15s:** “Are you missing from Google Search, or from Google Maps? That's two different problems.”
**15-28s:** “For your website, check the page in Search Console. For Maps, check whether your Business Profile is eligible and verified.”
**28-38s:** “Even if both are working, you might not rank for a competitive service search. That's where relevance and local competition matter.”
**38-45s:** “Our free nine-step troubleshooting guide helps you identify your next action.”

**On-screen text:** “Search ≠ Maps”; “Indexed ≠ ranking”; “Verified ≠ top position”; “Check before spending”.

**CTA:** Link in profile or platform-supported link to the canonical article. No ranking or time-to-result promises.

## Pilot B: Google Search Console shows impressions but no clicks

**Canonical article:** https://www.kydosdigital.com/blog/search-console-impressions-no-clicks.html

### B1: LinkedIn/Facebook carousel, 6 slides
**Audience:** Owner or marketing manager who has seen impressions increase in Search Console but doesn't know why enquiries haven't followed.

1. **Cover:** “Google says your site got impressions. Why are there no clicks?”
2. **Definition:** “An impression means your result appeared under Google's reporting rules. It doesn't mean the searcher wanted to visit.”
3. **Ranking:** “If your average position is around 20 or worse, improve relevance and visibility before obsessing over your meta description.”
4. **Search result:** “If you rank prominently for the right query, compare your real Google result with the intent behind the search.”
5. **Conversion:** “Clicks without enquiries? Check whether the page answers the query and whether forms, calls and analytics work.”
6. **Action:** “Use the free eight-field Search Console worksheet on the Kydos blog.”

**Caption draft:** “More Google impressions aren't the same as more customers. Before rewriting every page title, check the query, page, average position and actual search result. We've shared a practical worksheet to help small businesses work out what to fix first. #GoogleSearchConsole #SmallBusinessSEO #GreaterManchester #SEOAdvice #WebsiteTraffic”

**Design direction:** Use illustrative diagrams or an explicitly labelled fictional mini-table, not an unsourced screenshot or unqualified client performance figures. Explain that Search Console metrics vary by query, device, country and time.

**Evidence:** https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/snippet .

### B2: 30-40 second short-form explainer
**Hook (0-6s):** “10,000 Google impressions and hardly any clicks? The first fix might not be your title.”
**6-18s:** “Check your average position and the actual searches triggering your page. If you appear far down the results, people may never see your link.”
**18-30s:** “If you're appearing prominently for the right searches, check what Google is showing and whether your answer matches what people need.”
**30-40s:** “And if people do click but never enquire, investigate your website and tracking. Our free worksheet shows how.”

**On-screen text:** “Impressions ≠ visits”; “Position and intent first”; “Clicks ≠ enquiries”.

**CTA:** Link to the canonical article. “10,000” is an illustrative hook, not a claimed Kydos client result.
