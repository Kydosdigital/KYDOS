# Kydos Digital Team Profile Content Template

Status: Internal working standard

## Source of truth
The Airtable **Team Profiles** table is the canonical record for public staff information. Do not infer or invent names, titles, career history, qualifications, years of experience or sector background.

## Required profile fields
- Full display name
- Current Kydos job title
- Department / function
- Role at Kydos
- One-line bio, 15 to 25 words
- Short bio, 60 to 90 words
- Full bio, 120 to 200 words
- Industry experience
- Career background
- Core specialisms
- Tools and platforms
- Selected Kydos work
- Approved staff photo

## Optional fields
- Qualifications and certifications
- Years of experience
- Public location
- LinkedIn profile
- Professional memberships

## Full profile
### About [First name]
Explain what they actually do, the problems they solve and the experience they bring.

### Career background
Summarise relevant previous roles, disciplines and progression. Previous employers can be named only when accurate and appropriate.

### Industries worked across
List only genuine sector experience.

### Core specialisms
Use 3 to 6 specific capabilities.

### Selected Kydos work
Use 2 to 4 real Kydos responsibilities, campaigns or projects. Do not publish confidential results.

### Tools and platforms
Only tools the staff member genuinely uses.

### Qualifications and certifications
Optional and verified before publication.

## Writing rules
1. Write like a serious agency, not a CV generator.
2. Avoid “passionate professional”, “results-driven individual”, “dynamic expert”, “seasoned guru” and similar filler.
3. Do not make everyone sound like senior leadership.
4. Titles must reflect actual responsibility.
5. Keep previous-employer experience separate from Kydos work.
6. Do not attribute client results to a person unless their role supports that attribution.
7. Do not present freelance, contract or project work as permanent employment.
8. Use British English.
9. Keep the card concise and use the full profile for context.
10. Use the same approved name, title, photo and core facts everywhere.

## Photography standard
- 4:5 portrait canvas
- Neutral or white background
- Face and upper torso visible
- Consistent headroom
- Suggested master export: 800 × 1000 px
- Website format: WebP
- Use a consistent crop rather than changing the person's appearance
- Filename after identity confirmation: firstname-lastname-kydos.webp
- Alt text: “[Full Name], [Job Title] at Kydos Digital”

## Approval flow
Awaiting details -> Draft -> Management/staff review -> Approved -> Published
