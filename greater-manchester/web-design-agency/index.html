What changes the brief

Greater Manchester website buying considerations that matter before design starts.

The site needs to match the buying journey

A local service business may need strong location relevance and a fast quote route, while a B2B firm may need deeper service proof, expertise and consultation pathways. Structure should follow how customers decide, not a favourite template.

Mobile is a release gate, not a polish task

Customers will often arrive from Maps, search, social or ads on a phone. Navigation, forms, headings, comparison tables and contact actions must remain usable on smaller screens.

Ownership and integrations need to be clear

Before signing, confirm domains, hosting, CMS access, analytics, forms, CRM connections, licences and handover. A cheaper build can become expensive if the business does not control its own essential assets.

Localisation should be useful

A regional hub should help users reach genuinely relevant Manchester and borough pages. Borough pages should carry differentiated local buying context rather than repeating this page with a place-name substitution.
