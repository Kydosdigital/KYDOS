What changes the brief

Four decisions that matter for social media across Greater Manchester.

Content supply needs to be agreed

Before comparing packages, establish who provides photos, video footage, offers, approvals and subject-matter input. A content calendar can fail quickly when production responsibilities are vague.

Posting volume is not the whole service

Community management, page optimisation, reporting, paid media, CRM and lead follow-up change what a package actually does. Compare responsibilities and outcomes, not only posts per week.

Local relevance should not become cliché

A Greater Manchester business may serve one neighbourhood, several boroughs or the whole region. Content should reflect real customers, offers, service areas and commercial moments rather than generic location hashtags.

Lead handling can matter as much as reach

For businesses using paid social, speed of follow-up and CRM routing can affect whether enquiries become conversations. Pipeline and Partner include CRM and automation elements for this reason.
