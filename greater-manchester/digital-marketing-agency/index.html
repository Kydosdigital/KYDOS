Before you choose a provider

Greater Manchester buying considerations that should change the brief.

The point of regional localisation is not to repeat the same copy with a new place name. These decisions affect channel choice, implementation and measurement.

Channel mix should follow the bottleneck

A business that lacks qualified traffic needs a different plan from one that already has traffic but loses enquiries. Compare agencies on how they diagnose the problem before selling a bundle.

Local demand is not uniform

Manchester city centre, Trafford, Stockport, Bolton and the other boroughs can have different customer journeys, competitors and catchment areas. Localisation should change targeting and content where the evidence supports it.

Implementation ownership matters

Ask who edits pages, launches campaigns, configures tracking, routes leads and reports on outcomes. Strategy without implementation responsibility can leave important work between suppliers.

Measurement needs to connect to enquiries

Clicks and impressions are useful diagnostics, but decision-stage buyers should also understand how calls, forms, booked appointments, qualified leads and CRM outcomes will be tracked where systems allow it.

What does Kydos digital marketing include?

How much does digital marketing cost?

Is Kydos based in Greater Manchester?

Yes. Kydos is based in Manchester and serves businesses across Greater Manchester and the wider UK. We do not claim separate offices in each borough.

Should I use one agency for every channel?

Not automatically. One agency can help when channels need to work together, but specialist support can be appropriate for a narrow problem. Compare implementation responsibility, expertise, ownership and reporting before deciding.

Can Kydos work with an existing website or CRM?

Yes, where the existing systems are suitable. The first step is to understand what is already in place, what can be retained and what needs to change.

How does Kydos measure digital marketing?

Measurement depends on the channel and sales process. Kydos plans tracking around meaningful actions such as calls, forms, booked appointments, lead stages and revenue signals where the technology and data allow it.
