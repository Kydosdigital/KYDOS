What changes the brief

Greater Manchester PPC decisions that need to be made before launch.

Media spend and management are different costs

A proposal should clearly separate the budget paid to the advertising platform from the fee for strategy, setup, optimisation and reporting. Combining them hides what the agency is actually charging.

Geographic targeting needs commercial logic

A campaign may target the whole region, selected boroughs or a tighter service radius. Location settings should follow where the business can profitably serve customers, not a desire to make the campaign look bigger.

Landing-page responsibility should be explicit

Clicks cannot fix a weak destination. Agree whether the existing page is suitable, whether a dedicated landing page is needed and who owns copy, forms and conversion changes.

Conversion tracking is a buying criterion

Ask what counts as a conversion, how calls and forms will be measured, who owns the ad account and analytics access, and how lead quality will be reviewed after launch.
