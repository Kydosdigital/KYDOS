What changes the brief

Greater Manchester SEO buying considerations that are more useful than another audit checklist.

Local SEO needs real service-area logic

A business serving one borough should not automatically be given ten thin location pages. Expansion should follow genuine coverage, differentiated buying context and evidence of search demand.

Implementation beats audit volume

A long audit only matters if important fixes reach the website. Compare who owns technical changes, content implementation, internal linking and local page improvements.

Commercial pages need to earn their place

Provider, pricing, quote, package and comparison pages can help buyers, but overlapping variants can cannibalise one another. Kydos checks intent before adding another page.

Reporting should explain movement

Useful SEO reporting connects visibility, rankings, landing-page traffic and enquiries, while being clear about attribution limits and the time required for search changes to compound.

SEO across Greater Manchester

What is included in Kydos SEO?

The published Basic scope includes keyword research, on-page optimisation and monthly reporting. Advanced adds technical SEO plus off-page and link-building work. Enterprise adds local SEO, content creation and optimisation, and a dedicated strategist.

How much does SEO cost?

Kydos SEO is custom quoted. Scope depends on the current site, technical condition, competition, content requirements and geographic reach.

Does Kydos guarantee Google rankings?

No. Rankings cannot be guaranteed. Kydos focuses on controllable work such as technical improvements, page quality, internal linking, local relevance, content and authority building.

Can Kydos do local SEO across Greater Manchester?

Yes, where the business genuinely serves those areas. We avoid manufacturing location pages that only change place names.

How long does SEO take?

Timing varies with the website, competition, crawl and indexing, implementation speed and authority. Kydos does not promise one fixed timeline for every site.

Is Kydos based in Manchester?

Yes. Kydos is Manchester-based and serves Greater Manchester and wider UK clients without claiming offices in each borough.
