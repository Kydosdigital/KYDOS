What should an SEO agency inspect before recommending work?

A serious SEO proposal should start with the website you already have. Before adding new content or building links, the agency should understand whether Google can crawl the important pages, whether the correct pages are indexed, whether pages compete with each other, and whether the site answers the searches most likely to produce business.

Crawl and indexabilityRobots directives, XML sitemap, canonical tags, indexable URLs and accidental duplication.
Keyword-to-page mappingWhich commercial searches belong to which service, location and industry pages.
On-page relevanceTitles, H1s, headings, page copy, internal links, images and search intent.
Technical conditionBroken links, mobile usability, performance, duplicate URLs and structured data.
Local searchLocation relevance, local landing architecture and Google Business Profile support when local SEO is in scope.
Conversion pathWhether high-intent organic visitors can actually find the next step and make an enquiry.

What does technical SEO actually include?

Technical SEO is the part users often do not see, but search engines still rely on it. Kydos' published Advanced SEO scope explicitly includes technical fixes such as site speed and mobile usability.

Depending on the site, the technical review can also identify crawl barriers, duplicate URL patterns, broken internal links, incorrect canonical tags, poor sitemap coverage, structured-data errors and inefficient page architecture. Not every site needs every fix, which is another reason SEO is custom quoted.

Technical SEO should support useful pages, not replace them. A technically perfect site with thin service pages will still struggle to satisfy high-intent searches. Strong SEO connects technical foundations, relevant content, internal linking and conversion.

Local SEO is particularly important when customers search for a provider in or around Manchester. Searches such as “SEO agency Manchester”, “solicitor Manchester”, “dentist Manchester” or “roofing company Manchester” combine a service need with a location need.

Does SEO require content and landing pages?

Often, yes. Search engines need a relevant page to return for a query. If a business wants to rank for ten different services but has one generic “Services” page, the site may not provide enough depth or clarity for each commercial search.

Kydos' published Enterprise SEO scope includes content creation and optimisation for blogs and landing pages. The difference matters:

  • Landing pages should target commercial searches where the user is looking for a provider, price, service or location.
  • Supporting articles should answer comparisons, costs, objections and research questions, then internally link into the relevant commercial page.
  • Industry pages should show how the service changes for a particular sector rather than repeating generic SEO copy.

This is the architecture Kydos is using for its own Greater Manchester rollout: commercial service pages sit in a location hierarchy, while supporting articles feed relevance and internal links into those pages.

What could the first 90 days of SEO look like?

The exact work should follow the audit, but a sensible sequence is to fix the foundations before scaling content. A sample plan might look like this:

Sample 90-day Manchester SEO plan

  • Days 1–30: crawl and indexation review, baseline rankings and conversions, keyword-to-page map, technical priority list, canonical and sitemap checks, core service-page optimisation.
  • Days 31–60: improve priority commercial pages, build missing internal-link paths, address technical issues, strengthen local relevance and begin planned supporting content.
  • Days 61–90: review ranking and query movement, improve pages using live Search Console data, expand content around proven intent, develop off-page activity where included, and refine conversion paths.

The point is not that every website follows the same calendar. The point is that work should be sequenced and measurable rather than delivered as an unexplained monthly list.

What counts as proof when comparing SEO agencies?

SEO is full of claims that are difficult for a buyer to verify. Ask for evidence you can inspect: the scope of work, which pages were changed, what technical issues were fixed, which queries are improving, whether organic landing pages are producing enquiries, and what the next action is based on the data.

10 questions to ask before choosing an SEO agency in Manchester

  1. What will you audit before proposing work? The answer should be specific to your site.
  2. Which pages are the commercial priority? Traffic alone should not decide the plan.
  3. What is included in the quote? Clarify technical SEO, content, links, local SEO and reporting.
  4. Who implements technical fixes? Finding an issue and fixing it are different responsibilities.
  5. How will you avoid keyword cannibalisation? Ask how the agency maps one main intent to one preferred page.
  6. How will internal linking be planned? Important pages should not depend on an XML sitemap for discovery.
  7. How will local SEO differ from national SEO? Manchester-specific intent needs a genuine local strategy.
  8. How will progress be measured? Ask for visibility, clicks, landing-page performance and conversion metrics.
  9. Do you guarantee rankings? Be cautious with guaranteed number-one positions.
  10. What happens after the first 90 days? SEO should adapt to live data rather than repeat the same checklist indefinitely.

SEO or PPC: which should come first?

If your business needs faster search-demand testing, Google Ads can provide data quickly. If the goal is to build a durable organic acquisition asset, SEO deserves investment. In many cases the channels can work together: paid search identifies valuable queries while SEO builds longer-term pages around commercially proven intent.

If the website itself is weak, both channels can underperform. A good decision may be to improve the landing experience first rather than sending more traffic to a page that does not convert.
