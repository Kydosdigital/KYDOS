Do you need a Starter, Professional or Enterprise website?

A small service business may only need five strong pages if each one has a clear role. A growing multi-service business may need ten or more pages so individual services, industries and locations have enough depth. A company with internal workflows, account areas, dashboards or data integrations needs custom development rather than simply “more pages”.

The package should be chosen around content architecture and functionality, not the desire to have the largest website possible.

When a smaller website is enough

A Starter site can work well when the business has one clear offer, a simple buying journey and a small number of services. The pages still need to answer trust, service and conversion questions properly.

When a larger marketing site makes sense

Professional sites are useful when there are several services, location pages, industries, lead-generation forms or ongoing content needs. The published CRM integration and blog setup make this tier better suited to a wider digital-marketing system.

When custom development is justified

APIs, dashboards and advanced forms should solve real workflow problems. Custom code creates more capability but also more scope, testing and maintenance responsibility.

What does “SEO-ready web development” actually mean?

The Starter package includes basic SEO setup, but a search-friendly website is broader than inserting keywords after design is finished. Page hierarchy, clean navigation, crawl paths, metadata, mobile usability, internal links and indexable content all need to work together.

Clear page hierarchyServices, locations and supporting content should sit in a structure users and search engines can follow.
Indexable contentImportant service information should be accessible as crawlable page content.
Canonical controlEach page should signal its preferred URL consistently.
Mobile usabilityNavigation, forms and calls to action must work on smaller screens.
Internal linkingMoney pages should not depend on an XML sitemap as their only discovery path.
Performance awarenessImages, scripts and page structure should avoid unnecessary weight where possible.

Ongoing SEO is still a separate service. A website build can create good foundations, while the SEO programme continues with keyword targeting, technical improvements, local SEO, content and authority work.

How should a business website convert traffic?

A website can attract traffic from SEO, Google Ads, social media, referrals or direct visits. Each important page should make the next step obvious without forcing the visitor to hunt for a contact route.

For a service business, the conversion might be a form, call, consultation request or booking. For ecommerce it is a product purchase. For B2B it may be a discovery call. The layout should support the real buying action.

Do not confuse a homepage with a landing page. A homepage serves several audiences and routes. A campaign landing page can focus on one offer, one audience and one primary conversion action.

What should a web-development process include?

  1. Discovery: define audiences, services, conversion goals, integrations and content requirements.
  2. Architecture: plan navigation, page hierarchy and the role of each page before styling everything.
  3. Content and design: create page content and visual structure around the customer journey.
  4. Development: build responsive pages, forms, integrations and agreed functionality.
  5. Quality assurance: test mobile layouts, links, forms, metadata, crawl paths and key conversion journeys.
  6. Launch and handover: verify live URLs, indexing basics, analytics readiness and ownership.

10 questions to ask a Manchester web-development agency

  1. What is included in the quote? Pages, design, copy, integrations, revisions and support should be clear.
  2. Who owns the website and accounts? Confirm access and handover before work begins.
  3. Is the site mobile optimised? This should be a baseline requirement.
  4. What SEO setup is included? Basic setup and ongoing SEO are different scopes.
  5. Can you connect our CRM? Clarify the exact integration before development.
  6. Who supplies the content? A beautiful layout still needs substantial page copy.
  7. How will forms be tested? Conversion paths should be verified before launch.
  8. What happens to old URLs? Rebuilds need careful URL and redirect planning.
  9. What support is included after launch? Enterprise Kydos includes three months of ongoing support.
  10. How will the site support future SEO and campaigns? Architecture should leave room for services, locations, industries and landing pages.
