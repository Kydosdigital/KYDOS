When does Google Ads make sense?

Google Ads is strongest when people are already searching for the product or service. A Manchester solicitor, private clinic, training provider, trades business or B2B company can use paid search to appear against defined commercial queries rather than waiting for organic visibility to develop.

The quality of the campaign still depends on the details: search terms, match types, negatives, ad wording, location settings, landing pages and conversion tracking. Paying for clicks is not the goal. The goal is to buy relevant opportunities at a cost the business can sustain.

Keyword research should separate intent

Someone searching “what is PPC” is not the same buyer as someone searching “PPC agency Manchester” or a service-specific phrase with strong purchase intent. Budget should be concentrated around the searches most closely aligned with the commercial objective.

Where do Meta Ads and paid social fit?

Paid social can create or re-engage demand even when the audience is not actively searching. That makes it useful for visual services, offers, retargeting, lead-generation campaigns and businesses with strong creative assets.

Kydos' wider paid advertising offer includes Meta and multi-platform campaigns, while the Enterprise scope explicitly includes Google, Facebook and LinkedIn. The right mix depends on where the audience is, how the offer is explained and what conversion event can be measured.

Organic social and paid social are related but not identical. Organic social management builds consistent brand presence. Paid social buys distribution and requires a media budget, campaign setup, targeting, testing and conversion measurement.

What is the difference between the management fee and ad spend?

The agency fee pays for the work of planning, setting up, managing, testing and reporting on campaigns. Ad spend is the separate media budget paid to Google, Meta, LinkedIn or another platform.

When comparing Manchester PPC quotes, make sure both numbers are visible. A proposal that says “£1,000 per month” is ambiguous unless you know how much goes to media and how much goes to management.

Do paid ads need a landing page and tracking?

Paid media can only be judged properly when the journey after the click is measurable. The campaign may send users to an existing service page, a dedicated landing page, a lead form or an ecommerce product page. The important thing is that the destination matches the advert and makes the intended action obvious.

Message matchThe landing page should continue the same service, offer and intent as the advert.
Conversion actionForm, phone call, booking, purchase or another defined action.
Source trackingKnow which campaign, platform or keyword generated the conversion.
Lead qualityMeasure qualified opportunities, not only raw form submissions.

What should a PPC agency report?

Clicks and impressions show delivery, not business value. Depending on the campaign, useful reporting can include search terms, click-through rate, cost per click, conversion rate, cost per conversion, qualified lead rate, booked appointments, sales and return on ad spend.

For lead generation, raw cost per lead can be misleading. Ten cheap enquiries that never qualify can be worse than three more expensive leads that fit the service and close.

10 questions to ask before hiring a PPC agency in Manchester

  1. Which platforms will you manage? Google, Meta, LinkedIn and shopping require different skills.
  2. Is ad spend separate? Know the exact media budget and management fee.
  3. Who owns the ad account? Your business should understand where campaigns and history live.
  4. Which conversion will be optimised? A form submission, call, booking or purchase should be defined.
  5. How will search terms be controlled? Ask about keyword intent and negatives.
  6. What A/B testing is planned? Testing should have a clear variable and learning goal.
  7. Who creates the landing page? Clarify responsibility before launch.
  8. How will lead quality be fed back? The cheapest lead is not always the right lead.
  9. How often will campaigns be optimised? Enterprise Kydos scope includes weekly optimisation and reporting.
  10. What would make you pause or scale spend? The answer should be tied to data, not instinct.
