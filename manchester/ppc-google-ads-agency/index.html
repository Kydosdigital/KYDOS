The money-flow question

Management fee and ad spend are not the same cost.

If an agency quotes “£1,000 per month” without separating the fee from the actual platform budget, you cannot compare the proposal properly. One provider may mean £300 management plus £700 media; another may mean £1,000 management before a single pound reaches Google.

Kydos treats advertising spend separately. That makes it easier to see what is being paid to the platform and what is being paid for strategy, setup, optimisation and reporting.

Before you launch

Conversion tracking needs to be trustworthy before optimisation becomes meaningful.

A button click is not automatically a lead. A page view is not automatically a sale. Before campaigns scale, the account needs a clear definition of what counts as success and how that action is recorded.

Primary conversions

Actions that indicate genuine commercial progress.

  • Qualified form submission
  • Phone call
  • Booking
  • Purchase

Supporting signals

Useful behaviour that can help diagnose the journey without being treated as revenue.

  • Landing-page engagement
  • CTA clicks
  • Form starts
  • Key page visits

Lead quality

The account should eventually learn whether conversions are useful, not only whether they are cheap.

  • Service fit
  • Location fit
  • Sales qualification
  • CRM outcome where available
Manchester targeting

“Greater Manchester” is not a reason to target every postcode equally.

A campaign should reflect where the business can profitably fulfil work. A Manchester company may genuinely serve Salford, Stockport, Trafford and the rest of Greater Manchester, or it may only want customers within a tighter radius. Geography should be a commercial decision rather than a default setting.

What we would ask before setting locations

Where do your best customers currently come from? Which areas can you realistically serve? Are some services available in a wider area than others? Is the business trying to grow into a new borough, or simply prevent budget leaking outside its existing catchment?

Search terms & waste

A high click-through rate can still be a bad campaign.

If the search terms are irrelevant, the landing page is weak or the enquiries do not fit the business, good-looking platform metrics can hide poor commercial performance.

What to reviewWhy it matters
Search termsShows what people actually typed before the ad was triggered, not just the keyword you selected.
Negative keywordsHelps exclude jobs, research, unrelated services and other low-fit searches where appropriate.
Location performanceShows whether spend is producing useful activity in the areas the business can serve.
Device performanceCan reveal mobile landing-page or call-handling problems that are not obvious at account level.
Conversion qualityDistinguishes a cheap conversion from an enquiry the sales team would actually want.
Landing pages

Sometimes the PPC problem is not inside the ad account.

If a high-intent visitor clicks a relevant ad and lands on a page that is slow, vague, untrustworthy or difficult to contact, continued bid optimisation has a limit. Kydos also provides web and landing-page services, so the conversion journey can be addressed when the page itself is the bottleneck.

A paid landing page should answer

Why this service, why this provider, why now?

Clear offer, scope, proof, objections, location relevance and a simple next action matter more than stuffing the page with advertising keywords.

Before spending more

Fix obvious conversion friction first.

Forms, phone numbers, mobile layout, tracking, trust and offer clarity should be checked before scaling budget into a weak page.

What should be in the proposal?

A PPC proposal should make the commercial test easy to understand before any money is spent.

It should be possible to read the proposal and know what is being advertised, where the ads will run, what counts as a conversion, which campaign types are included, how often optimisation happens and which costs sit outside the management fee.

FAQ

Questions Manchester businesses ask before hiring a PPC agency.

How much does PPC management cost with Kydos in Manchester?

Kydos PPC services are custom quoted because campaign scope depends on platforms, campaign types, conversion tracking, landing pages and optimisation requirements. Advertising spend is separate from the agency management fee.

What is included in the Starter PPC package?

The published Starter PPC scope includes Google Ads campaign setup, keyword research and basic A/B testing.

What is included in the Comprehensive PPC package?

Comprehensive includes all Starter features plus advanced audience targeting and retargeting, and multiple campaign types including search, display and shopping.

What is included in Enterprise PPC?

Enterprise includes a dedicated PPC strategist, weekly optimisation and reporting, and multi-platform campaigns across Google, Facebook and LinkedIn.

Is Google Ads spend included in the management fee?

No. Advertising spend is separate. The client provides the media budget while Kydos manages the agreed campaign scope.

Can Kydos manage Meta or LinkedIn ads too?

Yes. Multi-platform paid media is included within the published Enterprise PPC scope, which can cover Google, Facebook and LinkedIn campaigns.

Will Kydos set up conversion tracking?

Tracking requirements are agreed in scope. Paid media should be measured against useful conversion actions such as qualified form submissions, calls, bookings or purchases rather than clicks alone.

Can Kydos build or improve landing pages for PPC?

Yes. Kydos offers landing page and web-development services. If an existing page is weakening campaign performance, the page can be reviewed and separately scoped rather than continuing to buy traffic into a poor conversion journey.

Do you guarantee a cost per lead?

No. Cost per lead depends on auction competition, demand, offer quality, targeting, landing-page performance and lead criteria. Kydos can optimise the controllable parts of the account but cannot responsibly guarantee one universal CPL.

How do you decide which keywords to target?

Keyword selection should reflect commercial intent, service relevance, geography and budget. High-intent terms are prioritised while negative keywords are used to reduce irrelevant searches where appropriate.

Do you only manage PPC for Manchester businesses?

No. Kydos is based in Manchester and manages digital marketing for businesses across Greater Manchester and the wider UK.
