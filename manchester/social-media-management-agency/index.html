The raw-content question

Who actually supplies the photos and video?

This is one of the most important questions to ask before signing any social media package. Kydos includes professional graphic design and video editing, but some content concepts still need genuine source material from the business: a team member speaking, a service being delivered, a product in use or a real customer environment.

During onboarding, we identify what already exists and what needs to be captured. A sustainable plan should not quietly depend on the owner filming five times a week if nobody agreed to that workload.

Our rule:

If a content idea requires something only your business can provide, we make that dependency clear rather than pretending “done for you” means we can manufacture real-world footage we do not have.

Manchester local content

Local relevance should come from the business, not repeated Manchester hashtags.

A Manchester social media strategy can include local projects, neighbourhood references, events, partnerships, customer questions and genuine service-area context. But not every post needs to announce the location. The strongest local content is usually the material that proves the business is active and relevant to the people it serves.

Serving Greater Manchester?

If customers also come from Salford, Stockport, Trafford, Bolton, Oldham, Rochdale, Bury, Wigan or Tameside, the content plan can reflect that footprint without turning every caption into a list of boroughs. Paid targeting, local proof and location-specific campaigns can be separated where there is a real commercial reason.

Content-only or lead system?

Choose based on what happens after someone engages.

Some businesses simply need a professional, consistent social presence. Others already get enquiries but lose them because response is slow or follow-up is inconsistent. That is why the higher tiers add ads, CRM, automation and nurture rather than merely increasing the number of posts.

FAQ

Questions Manchester businesses ask before outsourcing social media.

How much does social media management cost with Kydos in Manchester?

Kydos currently publishes four monthly social media plans: Starter at £165, Visibility at £275, Pipeline at £455 and Partner at £655. Ad spend is separate where paid ads management is included.

What is included in the £165 Starter plan?

Starter includes Instagram, Facebook and TikTok management, three posts per week, professional graphic design and video editing, a content calendar and strategy, plus Stories two to three times per week.

What is included in the £275 Visibility plan?

Visibility includes five posts per week, full page setup and optimisation, professional graphic design and video editing, content strategy/calendar, community management, Stories three to five times per week and a monthly report.

What is included in the £455 Pipeline plan?

Pipeline includes everything in Visibility plus paid ads management, CRM setup and management, instant lead automation and a monthly performance report. Advertising spend is provided separately by the client.

What is included in the £655 Partner plan?

Partner includes everything in Pipeline plus a dedicated landing page, multi-step email and SMS lead nurture, advanced CRM pipeline management and a website audit focused on conversion gaps.

Which platforms does Kydos manage?

The current Starter and Visibility social plans are built around Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Broader or different platform requirements can be discussed where they fit the business.

Does Kydos create graphics and videos?

Yes. Professional graphic design and video editing are included in the published Starter and Visibility scopes. Raw footage requirements depend on the content concept and should be agreed during onboarding.

Will I need to provide content?

Sometimes. The amount depends on the business. Service footage, team clips, customer environments or product demonstrations may need source material from the client, while Kydos handles planning, graphic design, editing, captions and the agreed publishing workflow.

Does Kydos reply to comments and messages?

Community management is included in the published Visibility package. The exact handling of comments, enquiries and sensitive messages should be agreed during onboarding.

Can Kydos manage social media and paid ads together?

Yes. Pipeline adds paid ads management to the Visibility package, while Partner adds a landing page, nurture and advanced CRM support. Advertising spend is separate.

Do you only manage Manchester businesses?

No. Kydos is Manchester-based and supports businesses across Greater Manchester and the wider UK.

How quickly can we start?

The onboarding timeline depends on how quickly brand information, account access, existing assets and approvals are supplied. Kydos confirms the working process during onboarding rather than promising the same launch date for every business.
