# Kydos Digital BOFU SEO Blog Strategy

## Objective
Build a UK-wide organic acquisition engine around high-commercial-intent searches, not generic traffic. The blog should attract business owners who are comparing agencies, services, budgets, channels and providers.

## Core rule
Do not mass-produce thin "[service] in [town]" pages. Regional content must have genuine local differentiation. Industry content must show real knowledge of the sector's customer journey, regulation, channel mix, economics and conversion points.

## Content architecture
1. National industry BOFU hubs
2. Service + industry articles
3. Cost/budget/comparison articles
4. Regional industry articles
5. Regional agency/service guides
6. Supporting MOFU evidence articles only where they strengthen BOFU clusters

## UK regional rollout
Use a hub-and-spoke model. Start with the largest commercial centres, then cover every UK nation and English region with unique local evidence.

Tier 1 cities:
London, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Liverpool, Bristol, Glasgow, Edinburgh, Cardiff, Belfast.

Tier 2:

## Internal linking
Every article should link to:
- the relevant service page
- pricing
- one parent industry hub
- two closely related articles
- a conversion CTA

## On-page requirements
- One primary intent per URL
- Unique title and meta description
- Canonical URL
- BlogPosting schema
- FAQ schema only when visible FAQs exist
- Strong H1 and useful H2 structure
- UK English
- Clear "updated" date
- Original local/industry information
- No keyword stuffing
- No invented case studies, reviews or performance claims

## Measurement
Track:
- organic landing sessions
- non-brand clicks and impressions
- top 10 / top 3 rankings
- enquiry CTA clicks
- form submissions
- qualified leads
- assisted conversions
- revenue/pipeline where CRM data allows

## Publishing cadence
Recommended starting cadence: 3 BOFU posts per week.
- 1 national industry/service article
- 1 regional industry article
- 1 cost/comparison/agency-selection article

At 3/week, one industry cluster can reach 10-12 substantial pages in a month without producing thin content.
