Landscaping Marketing Agency UK: Hiring Checklist

Garden design, hard landscaping and recurring maintenance are different businesses from a marketing perspective. Shortlist agencies that understand the distinction.

Updated 2 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guide

Start with project type and minimum fit

Tell providers whether you want design-led transformations, patios and hard landscaping, commercial grounds work or maintenance contracts. Define geography and the project sizes that make sense for your team.

Ask how seasonality changes acquisition

Demand and operational capacity can move through the year. Marketing should create the right pipeline at the right time, not simply maximise enquiries during periods when the diary is already full.

Inspect the portfolio workflow

Strong landscaping marketing depends on genuine visual evidence. Ask who captures or receives project assets, how before-and-after material is organised and how project pages support both search visibility and sales confidence.

Frequently asked questions

Should landscaping marketing separate design from maintenance?

Yes when the buyer, project value and sales process differ.

How should seasonality affect marketing?

Ask how the agency will plan content and budgets ahead of demand rather than react after capacity is full.

Are project galleries important?

For visual landscaping work, genuine completed-project evidence can help prospects assess fit and quality.

Should service areas be narrow?

They should reflect where your business can deliver profitably, which may vary by project size.

Who owns project photos?

Confirm rights and licences for photography and edited creative before signing.

What should reporting measure?

Useful project enquiries and downstream outcomes should sit above traffic-only metrics.
