Web Design Company Manchester Prices: Compare Quotes Before You Hire

If you are already comparing Manchester web design companies, the price only makes sense when you know exactly what the quote includes. A lower figure can be excellent value, or it can simply exclude copy, forms, SEO setup, integrations, revisions and support that another provider has included.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
For buyers already comparing suppliers. This guide focuses on provider selection, scope, ownership, pricing structure and commercial terms rather than beginner-level marketing education.

Turn every quote into the same checklist

Ask each shortlisted provider to price against the same page list, functionality and conversion goal. If one quote assumes three pages and another assumes ten, the totals are not comparable.

Use Kydos' web scopes as one market benchmark

Kydos Starter covers 3 to 5 pages, mobile optimisation, basic SEO setup and one revision round. Professional supports up to 10 pages, custom design, animations, forms, CRM/social integrations, blog setup and two revision rounds. Enterprise is for larger custom builds including APIs, advanced forms and dashboards, with a dedicated manager and three months of support.

Compare first-year cost, not only build cost

Hosting, premium plugins, maintenance, platform subscriptions and future support can change the real first-year spend. Ask whether any recurring cost is compulsory and which accounts the business owns.

Ask who owns the website and accounts

Ownership should cover the domain, hosting, CMS access, analytics and any paid software accounts. A cheap build is poor value if the business cannot move or manage the site later.

Check what happens if you need more pages

Ask what an extra service page, location page or campaign landing page costs after launch. A website designed to grow should not require a rebuild every time the business adds a service.

Compare support as part of the price

Clarify how bugs, urgent edits and future updates are charged. Support may be included for a period, sold separately or handled on demand.

Choose the quote with the fewest hidden assumptions

The strongest quote is usually the one that makes inclusions and exclusions easy to understand. Price transparency is useful only when the scope is equally transparent.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Kydos charge for web design?

Kydos currently custom quotes website projects because cost depends on page count, design, functionality and integrations.

What should a Manchester web design quote include?

At minimum, page scope, content responsibility, mobile design, SEO setup, forms, revisions, ownership, hosting/support and any recurring costs.

Is the cheapest web design quote usually the best value?

Not necessarily. Compare the deliverables and first-year costs before deciding.

Should hosting be included in a web design price?

It can be, but do not assume so. Ask whether hosting is included, optional or mandatory and who owns the account.

What is included in Kydos Starter web scope?

3 to 5 pages, mobile optimisation, basic SEO setup and one revision round.

How should I compare two web design companies?

Give both the same brief, then compare scope, exclusions, ownership, support and total cost rather than headline price alone.
