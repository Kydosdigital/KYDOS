Start with the commercial job, not the package name

A local business website should make it obvious what the company does, where it genuinely works and what a prospective customer should do next. The buying decision is therefore bigger than choosing the nicest visual style.

Stockport businesses often serve a mix of borough-wide, south Manchester and wider Greater Manchester customers. That makes service-area planning important: the agency should be able to separate genuinely local demand from searches that are better served by a broader Manchester or UK page.

Make the Stockport geography earn its place in the strategy

The strongest proposal should show how local credibility, service clarity and conversion tracking work together rather than treating Stockport as a keyword to paste into every page.

A useful local plan should be able to explain how Stockport town centre, Cheadle, Bramhall, Marple and surrounding areas fit into the real customer footprint. It should also be willing to leave some nearby places untargeted when there is not enough demand or operational reason to create separate content or campaigns.

The service-specific decision to challenge

For a Stockport business, the agency should decide whether one strong local service page is enough or whether separate service-area content is justified. That decision should come from the actual customer footprint, not from a desire to manufacture more URLs.

Ask what the agency will actually implement

Reports and recommendations are not the same as execution. Clarify which changes the agency can make directly, which depend on your own team, and which require another supplier. This is especially important when the work touches your website, analytics, advertising accounts or CRM.

Protect ownership and access

Your business should understand who owns the website, ad accounts, analytics properties, social profiles, creative files, CRM data and any third-party subscriptions used in delivery. Agree what happens to access and assets if you change provider later.

Make reporting useful for a Stockport business

Ask the agency to report against the action the business actually values, such as qualified calls, quote requests, consultations, bookings or sales. Local visibility matters, but it should be connected to a customer journey rather than treated as an end in itself.

Red flag before you sign

Watch for: The quote promises multiple town pages but gives no explanation of how those pages will differ or help a visitor make a decision.

What a sensible first 90 days should show

The provider should be able to state what it will audit, what it will change first, which commercial pages or campaigns receive priority, how tracking will be checked and what evidence would justify expanding the scope. That is more useful than a long list of activities with no order.
