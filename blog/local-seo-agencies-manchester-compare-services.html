Local SEO Agencies Manchester: Compare GBP, Location Pages & Reporting

If your customers choose providers by location, a general SEO retainer may not cover the local work you actually need. When comparing Manchester local SEO agencies, check exactly how Google Business Profile, local landing pages, reviews, citations and local conversion tracking fit into the service.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
Built for buyers, not beginners. This guide assumes you are already considering a supplier and focuses on scope, contracts, ownership, pricing structure and provider selection.

Ask how much of the service is Google Business Profile management

Clarify whether the agency optimises categories, services, descriptions, photos and business information, and whether ongoing profile updates or posts are included. GBP work should also match the information on your website.

Check the location-page strategy before paying for dozens of pages

Ask whether the agency creates pages only for genuine service areas with meaningful local intent. Thin pages that simply swap Manchester for another town can create a weak site architecture and little value for visitors.

Use real service areas, not every place on a map

A business serving Manchester, Salford, Stockport and Bolton may deserve different local content from a business that operates only in the city centre. The agency should ask where customers actually come from before creating the page plan.

Check how local SEO connects to the main website

Google Business Profile is only one part of the system. Service pages, internal links, contact information, local proof and site quality all support relevance and conversion once the user clicks through.

Ask about review strategy without buying fake reviews

A legitimate local SEO provider can help improve the process for requesting reviews from real customers. Avoid any service that proposes fabricated reviews or manipulative tactics that could put the profile at risk.

Choose the provider that can explain local lead quality

Map visibility and rankings matter, but the commercial outcome is whether the right local customers call, submit forms or book. Ask how the agency will connect local visibility to those actions.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between SEO and local SEO?

Local SEO adds location-focused work such as Google Business Profile, local pages, citations and local relevance to broader on-page and technical SEO.

Does Kydos offer local SEO?

Yes. Local SEO is included within the published Enterprise SEO scope.

Should every Greater Manchester town have its own page?

Not automatically. Pages should follow genuine service areas and search intent, with useful differentiated content.

Can an agency guarantee Google Maps rankings?

No credible agency controls Google's local results. Be cautious with guaranteed map-position promises.

Are citations still part of local SEO?

Consistent business information across relevant directories can support local visibility and trust, but citation volume should not replace stronger website and profile work.

What should local SEO reporting include?

Track local query visibility, Google Business Profile actions, traffic to local pages and useful conversions such as calls, forms or bookings.
