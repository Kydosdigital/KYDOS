Web Design Agencies Manchester: Compare Quotes, Scope & Support

If you are already collecting quotes from Manchester web design agencies, the useful question is no longer whether you need a website. It is whether two quotes that look similar on price are actually offering the same thing. Page count, copy, SEO setup, forms, CRM integration, revisions, post-launch support and ownership can change the value of a proposal dramatically.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
This is a hiring-stage guide. It assumes you already intend to outsource and focuses on how to compare providers, scope and commercial terms before you sign.

Start by making every agency quote comparable

Ask each shortlisted provider to quote against the same brief. If one agency is pricing five pages and another is quietly assuming ten, the headline figure tells you very little. Your comparison sheet should include page count, design approach, content responsibility, forms, integrations, SEO foundations, analytics, revision rounds, launch support and any recurring costs.

Compare the outcome, not just the design style

A good-looking portfolio matters, but a business website also has a job. Before hiring, decide whether the main action is a quote request, consultation, booking, phone call, product sale or application. Then ask the agency to explain how the proposed structure supports that action.

If the answer focuses only on colours, animations and page aesthetics, you still do not know whether the agency has thought about conversion.

Ask what happens to your existing SEO before a redesign

If you already have a live website, a redesign can damage search visibility when URLs change without a migration plan. Ask shortlisted agencies whether they will review existing indexed pages, map important old URLs, preserve useful content and check redirects before launch.

Decision-stage red flag: an agency proposes rebuilding the whole site but has not asked whether the current domain already receives organic traffic.

Clarify ownership before you sign

Before paying a deposit, establish who controls the domain, hosting account, analytics, CMS access and any third-party subscriptions. You should also know what happens if you move supplier later. A cheap initial build can become expensive if the business cannot access or transfer its own website.

Check the support model, not just launch day

Ask what is included after launch, how bugs are handled, whether content edits are chargeable, and what an urgent fix costs. If the site is business-critical, the support arrangement can matter more than a small difference in the original build fee.

Shortlist the agency that can explain trade-offs

The strongest proposal is not always the one promising the most features. It is often the one that can tell you what your business does not need yet. A credible agency should be able to separate essential launch requirements from nice-to-have functionality, then explain what can be added later without rebuilding everything.

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose the cheapest web design agency in Manchester?

Not automatically. Compare what the quote includes, the level of support, ownership, revisions, SEO setup and integration work. A lower price can still be good value if the scope genuinely matches what you need.

Do Manchester web design agencies usually publish prices?

Some publish fixed packages, while others custom quote because project complexity varies. Kydos custom quotes website builds but publishes the scope of its Starter, Professional and Enterprise options.

What should be included in a web design quote?

At minimum, clarify page count, design/build scope, content responsibility, mobile optimisation, forms, SEO setup, revisions, analytics, integrations, hosting or handover and post-launch support.

Should SEO be included in a website build?

Basic technical and on-page foundations should be considered during the build. Ongoing SEO content, authority work, local SEO and continuous optimisation are separate from basic setup.

How many web design agencies should I compare?

Three well-matched proposals are usually more useful than ten vague quotes. Give each agency the same brief so you can compare like for like.
