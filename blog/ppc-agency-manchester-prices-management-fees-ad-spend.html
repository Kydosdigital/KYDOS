PPC Agency Manchester Prices: Management Fees vs Ad Spend

PPC pricing is easy to misunderstand because two budgets sit side by side: the money paid to the advertising platform and the money paid to the agency. If you are comparing Manchester PPC providers, separate those figures before deciding which proposal is affordable.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
For buyers already comparing suppliers. This guide focuses on provider selection, scope, ownership, pricing structure and commercial terms rather than beginner-level marketing education.

Separate media spend from management immediately

If your total monthly budget is £2,000, ask exactly how much goes to Google or another platform and how much is the agency fee. A proposal that bundles both without explanation makes comparison harder.

Compare what the management fee actually covers

Check campaign setup, keyword research, negative keywords, ad copy, A/B testing, conversion tracking, audience work, landing-page advice, optimisation frequency and reporting.

Use Kydos' PPC levels as one benchmark

Kydos Starter PPC includes Google Ads setup, keyword research and basic A/B testing. Comprehensive adds advanced audience targeting/retargeting plus search, display and shopping. Enterprise adds a dedicated strategist, weekly optimisation/reporting and multi-platform support across Google, Facebook and LinkedIn.

Ask whether landing-page work is included

A strong campaign can still fail on a weak landing page. Clarify whether page recommendations, design or development sit inside the management fee or require separate scope.

Check setup fees and contract terms

Cost itemQuestion
SetupOne-off fee or included?
ManagementFlat fee, percentage or tiered?

Compare optimisation cadence

Ask how often campaigns are reviewed and what changes the agency typically makes. A higher fee can be justified by deeper account work, but only if the scope shows it.

Choose based on cost per useful outcome

The cheapest agency fee is not necessarily the best value if lead quality is poor. Compare how each provider intends to track forms, calls, bookings and qualified outcomes.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kydos include ad spend in PPC fees?

No. Kydos treats advertising spend as separate from agency management fees.

How does Kydos price PPC management?

PPC packages are custom quoted based on scope.

What should a PPC management fee include?

Campaign setup/management, keyword work, testing, optimisation, tracking and reporting should be clearly defined.

Should landing pages be included?

Do not assume so. Clarify whether landing-page recommendations or build work are part of the fee.

Is percentage-of-spend pricing better than a flat fee?

Neither is automatically better. Compare the actual work, incentives and total cost.

How should I compare two PPC proposals?

Separate ad spend from management, then compare setup, campaign types, tracking, optimisation, landing pages and reporting.
