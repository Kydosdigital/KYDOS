PPC Agency vs In-House Manchester: Who Should Manage Your Paid Search?

Once a business is spending consistently on Google Ads, the management model matters. An in-house person can be close to sales and product decisions, while an agency can provide broader specialist experience. The best choice depends on spend, complexity and internal capacity.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
For buyers already comparing suppliers. This guide focuses on provider selection, scope, ownership, pricing structure and commercial terms rather than beginner-level marketing education.

Choose in-house when paid search is a core daily function

An internal specialist can work closely with sales, pricing and promotions, respond quickly to commercial changes and spend all of their working time on your account.

Choose an agency when you need broader specialist depth

An agency can bring experience across campaign structures, industries, conversion tracking, landing pages and multiple platforms without requiring several hires.

Factor in recruitment and continuity

Hiring a strong PPC specialist takes time. An agency can start faster, while an internal hire can build deeper institutional knowledge. Consider what happens during holidays, sickness or staff turnover.

Use sales feedback as a deciding factor

If your internal team knows which leads are genuinely valuable, make sure that information reaches whoever manages paid search. Agency or in-house, optimisation should not stop at cost per form submission.

Choose the model that can make decisions quickly

Paid search loses efficiency when nobody can approve budget changes, landing pages or tracking fixes. The winning model is the one that combines expertise with fast access to business decisions.

When is in-house PPC better?

When paid media is large enough to justify a dedicated role and close daily access to commercial teams is important.
