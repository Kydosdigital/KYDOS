Catering Marketing Agency UK: Provider Selection Guide

A catering enquiry is only useful when the event type, date, location, headcount and commercial value fit the operation. Make that reality central to agency selection.

Updated 2 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guide

Segment the business before buying marketing

Wedding catering, office lunches, corporate events and private parties have different decision-makers and economics. Tell agencies which segments you want to grow, typical headcount, service radius and any minimum viable order or event value.

Check how enquiries will be qualified

A form that only asks for a name and email can create avoidable sales work. Ask how the journey can capture appropriate event information without making the enquiry experience unnecessarily difficult.

Use real food and event evidence

Visual quality matters, but imagery should reflect what customers can genuinely buy. Ask how the provider will manage approved photography, menus, venue collaborations and event content rather than filling channels with unrelated stock assets.

Ask how capacity changes campaigns

Catering has finite operational capacity. A provider should be able to adapt promotion around dates, segments or areas you need to fill rather than generating demand the kitchen cannot serve.

Frequently asked questions

Should corporate and wedding catering campaigns be separate?

Often yes because buyers, order values, lead times and repeat-purchase potential differ.

Should marketing show minimum spend?

Clear expectations can improve fit, but decide how pricing or minimums should be communicated within your sales process.

How should delivery radius be marketed?

Target genuine profitable service areas and adjust by event value where appropriate.

Who supplies food photography?

Agree who produces, licences, approves and publishes real imagery.

Should repeat corporate business be measured?

Where CRM data allows, repeat value can materially change how a lead source should be judged.

How should agencies be compared?

Compare segment targeting, enquiry qualification, visual production, landing pages, tracking and ownership.
