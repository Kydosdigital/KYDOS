Ready to contact providers? This guide is designed for the stage where you are deciding who deserves a discovery call or proposal request.

Start by deciding what “local” actually needs to mean

Salford is close enough to Manchester that many agencies market into both areas, which makes verification important. A provider can rank for a Salford search without being locally based or understanding the differences between borough-level demand and wider Manchester intent.

For a Salford business, local fit should mean more than a Salford keyword on the agency website. The supplier should be able to discuss your actual service radius, whether Eccles, Swinton, Worsley or wider Manchester matter commercially, and how that geography changes its recommendation.

Do not confuse local rankings with local delivery

A provider appearing for a Salford search has demonstrated some visibility, but that does not prove who will manage your account, whether work is outsourced or whether the team understands your commercial priorities. Verify the company, delivery team and scope separately.

Ask for proof that matches the service

Ask the provider to walk through the first campaign structure, conversion actions and search-term review process. You do not need confidential client numbers to judge whether the optimisation process is disciplined.

Use the discovery call to test judgement

Give every shortlisted agency the same brief. Ask what it would prioritise in the first 30–90 days, what it thinks is unnecessary right now and what information it still needs before making a recommendation. The strongest answer should show restraint as well as ideas.

Compare working style, not only capability

Ask who your day-to-day contact will be, how approvals work, how often strategy is reviewed and what happens when you disagree with a recommendation. A capable supplier can still be a poor fit if communication and decision-making do not match the way your business operates.

Check ownership and exit terms before the final decision

Confirm who owns the website, advertising accounts, analytics, social profiles, creative files, CRM data and any other assets touched during delivery. Also check minimum term, cancellation notice and what happens to access when the relationship ends.

Frequently asked questions

Does the agency need to be physically based in Salford?

No. Local presence can be useful for trust and context, but capability, delivery model and commercial fit should decide the shortlist.

How many PPC agencys should I compare?

Three is often enough to expose meaningful differences in scope, implementation, pricing and working style without turning the process into a large procurement exercise.

Should I choose the provider with the strongest local ranking?

Not automatically. Its own visibility can be a useful signal, but you still need to assess whether its service model matches your goals and whether it can implement what it recommends.

What should I ask on the first call?

Ask what the provider would prioritise first, what it would deliberately not do yet, what you need to supply, how success will be measured and what ownership remains with your business.

What is the biggest shortlist red flag?

The agency talks mainly about clicks, impressions and automation but cannot explain how it will judge lead quality.
