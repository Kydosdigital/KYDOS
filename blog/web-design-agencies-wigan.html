Buyer-stage guide. Start with what you need the supplier to own, implement and measure.

Start with the commercial objective

Wigan businesses can have a very different customer radius from firms closer to central Manchester. A useful local strategy should reflect whether the business mainly serves the borough, nearby towns or a broader North West market rather than forcing Manchester language into every campaign.

Before requesting proposals, decide which enquiries, services or customer segments matter most. That gives every agency the same brief and makes generic packages easier to challenge.

Make Wigan earn its place in the plan

A business focused on Wigan town centre, Leigh, Atherton, Hindley, Standish and surrounding parts of the borough may need a different structure from one serving all of Greater Manchester. The provider should explain why each local page, campaign or targeting choice exists.

Challenge the service-specific assumption

The agency should decide whether one strong Wigan page is enough or whether separate service-area content is justified by the real customer footprint.

Ask what the agency will actually implement

Recommendations are not execution. Clarify what the agency changes directly, what depends on your team, and what needs another supplier. This matters when the work touches the website, analytics, advertising accounts or CRM.

Protect ownership and access

Your business should understand who owns the website, ad accounts, analytics properties, social profiles, creative files, CRM data and third-party subscriptions. Agree what happens to access if you change supplier.

Demand useful reporting

Reporting should connect activity to the action the business values, such as qualified calls, quote requests, consultations, bookings or sales. Local visibility is useful only when it supports a customer journey.

Ask how the website will represent your service area without becoming repetitive

Give each web agency the same information about your priority services and where customers actually come from. If you serve Wigan town centre, Leigh, Atherton, Hindley and Standish, ask whether that needs one strong borough-level page, contextual references on service pages or a smaller number of genuinely distinct local pages. The answer should follow the customer journey and search intent, not a fixed page quota. Also confirm how calls, forms and mobile journeys will be tested before the new site is signed off.

What should the first 90 days show?

The provider should state what it will audit, what it will change first, which commercial pages or campaigns receive priority, how tracking will be checked and what evidence would justify expanding the scope.

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose a Wigan-based agency?

Local presence can help with context, but capability, implementation and commercial fit should decide the shortlist. Kydos is Manchester-based and works across Greater Manchester.

What should I send when asking for a proposal?

Share your current website or accounts, priority services, the areas you genuinely serve, your main conversion action and any existing tracking or CRM setup.

How do I compare two proposals?

Compare scope, implementation responsibility, ownership, reporting, exclusions, contract terms and the outcomes each provider is expected to improve.

Should the strategy target only Wigan?

Not necessarily. Target geography should match where the business can realistically serve customers.

What is a warning sign before signing?

A provider uses the location heavily in the proposal but cannot explain what actually changes in the strategy because of that geography.
