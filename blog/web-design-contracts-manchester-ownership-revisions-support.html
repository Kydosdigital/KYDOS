Web Design Contracts Manchester: Ownership, Revisions & Support Before Signing

Once you have chosen a preferred web designer, the contract determines what happens when scope changes, deadlines slip, revisions expand or the relationship ends. Before signing a Manchester web design agreement, make sure ownership and delivery responsibilities are explicit.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
For buyers already comparing suppliers. This guide focuses on provider selection, scope, ownership, pricing structure and commercial terms rather than beginner-level marketing education.

Start with ownership

The contract should make clear who owns the finished website, domain, design files, code where relevant, analytics, hosting and third-party accounts. Your business should know what it can take with it if the supplier relationship ends.

Define revision rounds precisely

Ask what counts as one revision round, how feedback should be submitted and what happens when additional changes are requested. Kydos publishes one revision round for Starter and two for Professional.

Clarify content responsibility

Website projects often stall because both sides assume the other is supplying the copy, images or legal text. The contract should state what the client provides and what the agency creates.

Check payment milestones

Contract areaWhat to clarify
DepositWhen does work formally start?
MilestonesWhat triggers each payment?
Final balanceIs it due before launch or handover?
Late feedbackCan delays change the timeline or fee?
Extra scopeHow are change requests quoted?

Review hosting and maintenance terms

Check whether hosting is compulsory, whether the site can be moved, what maintenance is included and whether security/plugin updates are your responsibility after launch.

Look for a clear termination and handover process

The contract should explain what happens if either party ends the project early, what work is handed over, what fees remain payable and how account access is transferred.

Do not sign until exclusions are visible

A good contract should make the boundaries as clear as the deliverables. Ask for anything vague to be clarified before paying a deposit.

Frequently asked questions

Who should own the website after it is built?

The agreement should state ownership clearly. Your business should understand its rights to the website, domain, accounts and assets.

How many revisions does Kydos include?

Kydos Starter includes one revision round and Professional includes two.

Should a web design contract include hosting?

It should at least state whether hosting is included, optional or your responsibility.

What happens if I add pages during the build?

Additional scope should be treated as a change request with a clear cost and timeline impact.

Should I sign before seeing a full scope?

You should have a clear written scope, commercial terms and responsibilities before committing.

Why does contract detail matter for SEO?

If URLs, content or migration responsibilities are unclear, a redesign can create avoidable search problems.
