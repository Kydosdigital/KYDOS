The best-fit PPC agency should be able to explain exactly what it will pay to target, how it will measure a useful conversion and how quickly it can identify wasted spend.

Ready to contact providers? This guide is designed for the stage where you are deciding who deserves a discovery call or proposal request.

Start by deciding what “local” actually needs to mean

Bury's catchment can vary sharply between areas such as Prestwich and Whitefield, which overlap strongly with north Manchester, and places such as Ramsbottom or Radcliffe. A shortlist should test whether the agency understands those differences rather than applying one broad local formula.

The best-fit Bury provider should be able to show where borough-level targeting makes sense, where nearby Manchester demand matters more and how that choice changes content, campaigns and reporting.

Do not confuse local rankings with local delivery

A provider appearing for a Bury search has demonstrated some visibility, but that does not prove who will manage your account, whether work is outsourced or whether the team understands your commercial priorities. Verify the company, delivery team and responsibilities separately.

Ask for proof that matches the service

Ask the provider to walk through the first campaign structure, conversion actions and search-term review process.

Use the discovery call to test judgement

Give every shortlisted agency the same brief. Ask what it would prioritise in the first 30–90 days, what it thinks is unnecessary right now and what information it still needs before making a recommendation. A strong answer should show restraint as well as ideas.

Compare working style, not only capability

Ask who your day-to-day contact will be, how approvals work, how often strategy is reviewed and what happens when you disagree with a recommendation.

Compare the final two proposals on total responsibility

When your shortlist is down to two, write down every activity required for success and mark who owns it: agency, client or third party. This exposes gaps around content, development, tracking, creative production and lead follow-up.

Check ownership and exit terms before the final decision

Confirm who owns the website, advertising accounts, analytics, social profiles, creative files, CRM data and other assets touched during delivery. Also check minimum term, cancellation notice and what happens to access when the relationship ends.

Frequently asked questions

Does the agency need to be physically based in Bury?

No. Local presence can help with trust and context, but capability, delivery model and commercial fit should decide the shortlist.

How many PPC agencys should I compare?

Three is usually enough to expose meaningful differences in scope, implementation, pricing and working style.

Should I choose the provider with the strongest local ranking?

Not automatically. Its own visibility is a useful signal, but you still need to assess whether its service model matches your goals.

What should I ask on the first call?

Ask what the provider would prioritise first, what it would deliberately not do yet, what you need to supply and how success will be measured.

What is the biggest shortlist red flag?

The agency talks mainly about clicks, impressions and automation but cannot explain how it will judge lead quality.
