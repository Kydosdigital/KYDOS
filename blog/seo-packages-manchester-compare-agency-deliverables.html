SEO Packages Manchester: Compare Agency Deliverables Before You Buy

SEO package names such as Basic, Growth and Premium are not useful unless you can see what changes on your website each month. If you are already comparing Manchester SEO packages, turn each proposal into a deliverables table before deciding which retainer is actually better value.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
Built for buyers, not beginners. This guide assumes you are already considering a supplier and focuses on scope, contracts, ownership, pricing structure and provider selection.

Compare implementation, not package labels

Ask how many priority pages receive on-page work, whether technical issues are implemented or only reported, how much content is produced, what off-page work is included and whether local SEO is part of the retainer.

Kydos' three SEO levels show how scope changes

Basic SEO includes keyword research, on-page optimisation and monthly reporting. Advanced adds off-page optimisation/link building and technical SEO fixes, including site speed and mobile usability. Enterprise adds local SEO, content creation/optimisation for blogs and landing pages, plus a dedicated SEO strategist. All are custom quoted.

Ask what is not included

Development time, large-scale content production, digital PR and specialist ecommerce work may sit outside a standard SEO retainer. A clear exclusions list makes comparison easier and reduces surprise costs.

Match the package to the current bottleneck

If your website has serious technical problems, a content-only package may not be the right first investment. If the site is technically sound but lacks commercial pages, another audit may add less value than implementation.

Check how success is measured

Ask whether reporting focuses on priority commercial queries, relevant pages, enquiries and other conversion actions rather than only total traffic and a long list of rankings.

Do not choose a bigger package simply because it has more line items

The best package is the one that solves the next constraints in the right order. A credible provider should be able to explain why certain activities are unnecessary at the start.

Frequently asked questions

How much are Kydos SEO packages?

Kydos currently custom quotes SEO based on scope rather than publishing one fixed fee for every business.

What is included in Basic SEO?

Kydos Basic SEO includes keyword research, on-page optimisation and monthly performance reporting.

What does Advanced SEO add?

Advanced adds off-page optimisation/link building and technical SEO fixes such as site speed and mobile usability.

What does Enterprise SEO add?

Enterprise adds local SEO, content creation/optimisation and a dedicated SEO strategist.

Should I choose the package with the most content?

Not automatically. Content volume should follow the commercial opportunity and the website's current technical condition.

How should I compare SEO packages from different agencies?

Translate every proposal into the same deliverables table and compare implementation, content, technical work, local SEO, links, reporting and exclusions.
