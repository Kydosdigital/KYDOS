Why the headline price is not enough

Paid-search pricing should always separate the money paid to the agency from the money paid to Google or another advertising platform. Without that distinction, two proposals can appear cheaper or more expensive than they really are.

Tameside businesses often serve a practical catchment that overlaps with east Manchester, Stockport and nearby boroughs. A useful pricing comparison should show whether the supplier is charging for genuinely different local work or simply applying the same scope to more place names.

Make Tameside justify any extra local scope

A Tameside business should ask whether the proposed budget is concentrated on the strongest local service-and-location combinations or spread too thinly across Greater Manchester.

Compare what happens after the first month

A useful quote should describe both setup and ongoing review. Ask what the provider will change each month, what counts as out-of-scope work and how the plan will respond when one area, service or channel performs better than another.

Check third-party costs separately

Software subscriptions, advertising spend, premium plugins, CRM licences, stock assets or specialist production can sit outside an agency retainer. Ask for these to be listed separately so the monthly figure reflects the true cost of ownership.

Check ownership before paying a setup fee

Your business should understand who owns the domain, website, ad accounts, analytics properties, creative files, CRM data and other assets created during the engagement.

Compare the fee against the complexity of the campaign

A local PPC account targeting one or two services in Tameside is not the same workload as an account covering many services, locations, campaigns and landing pages. Ask whether the management fee changes with account complexity and what triggers an increase. If the campaign covers Ashton-under-Lyne, Hyde, Denton, Stalybridge, Droylsden and wider Greater Manchester, the agency should explain how that geography is structured and how it will prevent budget from drifting into lower-value searches or areas the business cannot serve efficiently.

Red flag before you sign

Watch for: The quote gives one total monthly number but does not clearly separate agency management from advertising spend or explain conversion tracking.

How to shortlist the final two providers

Score clarity of scope, implementation responsibility, local relevance, reporting, ownership, contract flexibility and total first-year cost. Best value is the proposal that most directly solves the commercial problem, not automatically the lowest monthly fee.

Frequently asked questions

How much should ppc agency cost in Tameside?

There is no single fair price without knowing the scope. Compare deliverables, implementation responsibility, ownership, reporting and contract terms rather than using the headline fee alone.

Should I choose the cheapest provider?

Not automatically. A cheaper quote can exclude implementation, creative, tracking or support that you later need to buy separately.

Should local targeting cost more?

Only when it creates genuinely distinct work such as separate campaigns, pages, creative or tracking.

What should a quote show clearly?

Included deliverables, exclusions, third-party costs, implementation responsibility, ownership, contract terms and reporting.

What is a pricing red flag?

The quote gives one total monthly number but does not clearly separate agency management from advertising spend or explain conversion tracking.
