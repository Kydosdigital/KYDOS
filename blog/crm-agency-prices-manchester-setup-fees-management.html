CRM Agency Prices Manchester: Compare Setup Fees, Management & Hidden Costs

Updated 2 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guide
Supplier-selection guide: this assumes you are already considering outside help for CRM implementation and management.

Start with the business problem

CRM quotes are difficult to compare because one provider may price only the build while another includes discovery, migration, automation, training and ongoing management.

Compare the first-year total cost, not just the setup fee. Include platform licences, paid connectors, messaging charges, maintenance and any per-user costs outside the agency invoice.

Ask who is responsible for implementation

Ask every agency to split the proposal into one-off implementation, recurring software, optional support and usage-based charges. This exposes hidden assumptions before you sign.

Do not assume a strategy, audit or setup line automatically includes every practical change needed to make it live. Put dependencies and internal responsibilities into the proposal.

Agree how success will be measured

Value should be judged against the operational problem being solved. If the CRM reduces forgotten leads and creates a reliable pipeline, that is more meaningful than simply replacing a spreadsheet with a more expensive database.

Decide the reporting model before delivery so both sides know which numbers are useful and which are merely diagnostic.

Protect ownership and handover

Keep appropriate business ownership of platforms, data and core accounts. Document administrator access, exports and what happens if the supplier relationship ends.

Ask for proof that matches the work

Ask for an itemised implementation scope and every recurring platform or connector cost before approval.

Where client information is confidential, a supplier can still demonstrate process quality through redacted documents, owned properties or implementation evidence without inventing outcomes.

Watch for this red flag

Red flag: A quote says 'CRM setup included' but does not state users, pipelines, workflows, integrations, migration or support.
