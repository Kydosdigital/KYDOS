Financial Adviser Marketing Agency UK: How to Choose

If you are already shortlisting marketing providers for an advice firm, compare how they handle trust, compliance workflow, lead quality and implementation, not just channel lists.

Updated 2 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guide

Start with the advice business you actually want to grow

A firm focused on retirement planning has a different acquisition brief from one targeting business owners, protection clients or higher-value investment relationships. Give shortlisted agencies the priority services, geography, client profile and enquiry criteria before comparing proposals.

Make compliance workflow part of the buying decision

Financial-services content cannot be treated like generic lifestyle copy. Ask who drafts content, who supplies source material, where your internal approval sits and how revisions are handled. A provider should be comfortable working inside your review process rather than treating compliance as a final-minute obstacle.

Compare implementation, not strategy slides

Ask how the provider will earn trust before the enquiry

Prospective clients may compare advisers for weeks before making contact. Service pages, adviser credentials, process explanations, useful answers and consistent local or niche positioning can matter as much as the first ad. Ask agencies how they will support the whole decision journey.

Do not accept traffic as the final KPI

Agree what a useful enquiry looks like and how marketing data will connect to consultations or other meaningful outcomes where possible. Search visibility and traffic are useful diagnostics, but they are not the same as commercially suitable demand.

Check cancellation and ownership before signing

Confirm notice periods, access to creative assets, website content, tracking configurations, advertising accounts and data. The cleanest time to resolve ownership is before the engagement starts.

Frequently asked questions

Should a financial adviser marketing agency understand compliance review?

Yes. Ask how drafts, claims and promotions move through your firm's approval process before publication.

Should we choose SEO or paid search first?

It depends on your services, geography, current visibility, budget and speed required. Ask providers to justify the sequence.

Who should own advertising and analytics accounts?

Your firm should retain clear access to core accounts and understand what happens when the engagement ends.

What should reporting include?

Reporting should connect marketing activity to qualified enquiries and agreed business outcomes where tracking allows.

Can an agency qualify financial advice leads?

Marketing can improve targeting and enquiry forms, but agree what information is appropriate to collect and how leads are handed to your team.

How should we compare agency retainers?

Compare implementation responsibility, channel scope, content production, compliance workflow, tracking and ownership rather than package names alone.
