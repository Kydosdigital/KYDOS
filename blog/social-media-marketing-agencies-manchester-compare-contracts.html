Social Media Marketing Agencies Manchester: Compare Contracts, Scope & Ownership

Once you have shortlisted social media agencies, the contract matters almost as much as the content. Before signing, check what the agency owns, what you own, how approvals work, whether advertising is separate and what happens to assets and access when the relationship ends.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
Built for buyers, not beginners. This guide assumes you are already considering a supplier and focuses on scope, contracts, ownership, pricing structure and provider selection.

Read the minimum term and notice period first

A low monthly fee can feel very different when tied to a long minimum commitment. Check minimum term, cancellation notice, renewal rules and whether the agency can increase fees during the agreement.

Clarify who owns creative files and account access

Your business should retain access to its social accounts and understand what happens to graphics, edited video and source files when the contract ends. Ask whether assets can be reused and downloaded after cancellation.

Check the approval liability

The contract should make clear who is responsible for factual claims, regulated content and final approval where required. An efficient process should not mean publishing sensitive business information without review.

Separate organic and paid-media obligations

If ads are included, ask who owns the ad account, who pays media spend, how tracking is handled and whether landing-page work is part of the contract. Organic management and paid acquisition are different services.

Choose terms that fit the working relationship you want

Some businesses prefer a long partnership with stable planning; others want flexibility while testing a new supplier. There is no universal best contract, but the commercial terms should be obvious before onboarding begins.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kydos require a long-term social media contract?

The current published Kydos social offer is designed without a long-term lock-in contract, with scope and billing terms confirmed before work starts.

Who should own social media accounts?

The business should retain clear access and control of its accounts. Clarify permissions rather than transferring permanent ownership to a supplier.

Who owns graphics and videos created by an agency?

Ownership and reuse rights should be stated in the agreement. Ask before signing rather than assuming.

Is ad spend included in a social media retainer?

With Kydos, paid advertising spend is separate from agency management. Ask every provider to separate media budget from fees.

What should cancellation terms cover?

Notice period, final publishing schedule, access removal, outstanding invoices, asset handover and ongoing ad campaigns should all be clear.
