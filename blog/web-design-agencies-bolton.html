Start with the commercial job, not the package name

A local business website should make it obvious what the company does, where it genuinely works and what a prospective customer should do next. The buying decision is therefore bigger than choosing the nicest visual style.

Bolton businesses may compete for customers within the borough, across north-west Greater Manchester or further afield depending on the service. A good local strategy should reflect that real catchment rather than treating every nearby town as a separate keyword opportunity.

Make the Bolton geography earn its place in the strategy

For businesses with a defined service radius, the agency should be able to show where Bolton-specific demand deserves dedicated attention and where broader Greater Manchester targeting is more commercially sensible.

A useful local plan should explain how Bolton town centre, Farnworth, Horwich, Westhoughton and surrounding parts of the borough fit into the real customer footprint. It should also be willing to leave some nearby places untargeted when there is not enough demand or operational reason to create separate content or campaigns.

The service-specific decision to challenge

For a Bolton business, the agency should decide whether one strong local service page is enough or whether separate service-area content is justified. That decision should come from the actual customer footprint, not from a desire to manufacture more URLs.

Ask what the agency will actually implement

Reports and recommendations are not the same as execution. Clarify which changes the agency can make directly, which depend on your own team, and which require another supplier. This is especially important when the work touches your website, analytics, advertising accounts or CRM.

Protect ownership and access

Your business should understand who owns the website, ad accounts, analytics properties, social profiles, creative files, CRM data and any third-party subscriptions used in delivery. Agree what happens to access and assets if you change provider later.

Make reporting useful for a Bolton business

Ask the agency to report against the action the business actually values, such as qualified calls, quote requests, consultations, bookings or sales. Local visibility matters, but it should be connected to a customer journey rather than treated as an end in itself.

Red flag before you sign

Watch for: The quote promises multiple town pages but gives no explanation of how those pages will differ or help a visitor make a decision.

What a sensible first 90 days should show

The provider should state what it will audit, what it will change first, which commercial pages or campaigns receive priority, how tracking will be checked and what evidence would justify expanding the scope.

What should I send when asking for a proposal?

How do I compare two proposals?

Put both proposals into the same checklist for scope, implementation, ownership, reporting, exclusions, contract terms and the outcomes the provider is responsible for improving.

Should the strategy target only Bolton?

Not necessarily. Target geography should match where the business can realistically serve customers. A provider should explain when Bolton, wider Greater Manchester or UK-wide intent is appropriate.
