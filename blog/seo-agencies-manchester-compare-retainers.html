SEO Agencies Manchester: Compare Retainers, Deliverables & Reporting

If you are already speaking to SEO agencies in Manchester, the hardest part is rarely finding a provider. It is working out whether two monthly retainers are actually comparable. One proposal may include technical work, content and local SEO, while another is mostly reporting and recommendations.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
This is a hiring-stage guide. It assumes you already intend to outsource and focuses on how to compare providers, scope and commercial terms before you sign.

Turn every SEO proposal into a deliverables table

Ask the agency to separate recurring work from one-off setup. Keyword research, technical fixes, content production, link acquisition, Google Business Profile work, reporting and strategy may all be described as “SEO management”, but the labour and value are very different.

SEO areaWhat to clarify
Keyword researchIs this initial only or refreshed as new opportunities appear?
On-page SEOHow many priority pages are actually optimised each month?
Technical SEODoes the agency implement fixes or only send recommendations?
ContentHow many pages/articles are created or improved?
LinksWhat type of off-page work is included and how is quality assessed?
Local SEOAre location pages and Google Business Profile included?

Do not buy reporting as if it were optimisation

Monthly dashboards are useful, but reporting is not the work itself. Ask what will physically change on the website during an average month. A good answer should name pages, issues, content or experiments rather than only ranking reports.

Ask who implements technical fixes

An audit that identifies 50 issues has limited value if nobody is responsible for implementing them. If the agency does not have development access or technical capability, establish whether your own developer must do the work and whether that cost sits outside the retainer.

Compare SEO around commercial pages, not traffic alone

For a business ready to invest, the objective should be visibility for searches that can create enquiries or sales. Ask which service, location and bottom-of-funnel pages the agency would prioritise first and why. Large traffic projections from broad informational content are not automatically better.

Challenge guarantees before signing

No agency controls Google's rankings. Be cautious with guaranteed position-one promises, especially when the provider cannot explain the query set, competition, current website authority or timeline assumptions.

Choose the retainer that matches your bottleneck

If your site has strong technical foundations but weak commercial content, paying primarily for another audit is unlikely to be the best fit. If the site has indexing and speed problems, a content-heavy retainer may not fix the first constraint. The proposal should reflect your current site, not a generic package.

Frequently asked questions

How much do SEO agencies in Manchester charge?

Pricing varies by competition, website size and scope. Kydos currently custom quotes SEO rather than publishing one universal monthly price.

What should an SEO retainer include?

It should clearly define the work across research, on-page optimisation, technical SEO, content, off-page work, local SEO and reporting as relevant to your goals.

Should an SEO agency create content?

If content is part of the strategy, clarify whether the agency writes, edits and uploads it or only provides briefs.

How long should I sign an SEO contract for?

SEO often needs sustained work, but contract terms are a commercial decision. Ask what the agency expects to complete at 30, 90 and 180 days before agreeing to a long commitment.

What should an SEO report show?

Commercially useful reporting should connect visibility and traffic to priority pages, enquiries or other meaningful conversion actions rather than only showing rankings.
