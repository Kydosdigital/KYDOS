How to Choose an Aesthetic Clinic Marketing Agency in the UK

The best agency for an aesthetic clinic is not necessarily the one with the biggest portfolio. It is the one that can explain how it will attract suitable prospects, stay within advertising rules, measure quality and improve the whole enquiry journey.

Updated 1 September 2026Commercial-intent guideUK

Start with your actual bottleneck

If the clinic is invisible in search, the problem is different from a clinic generating leads that nobody follows up quickly. Before comparing agencies, decide whether the priority is discovery, trust, lead volume, conversion, retention or measurement.

Ask these questions before signing

  1. Which treatment, audience or location would you prioritise first?
  2. What will you measure beyond clicks and followers?
  3. How do you handle CAP/ASA restrictions for cosmetic interventions?
  4. Who creates landing pages and fixes website conversion issues?
  5. What happens to a lead after the form is submitted?
  6. How often will strategy change based on performance?
  7. What is included in the management fee and what is paid separately?

Be careful with guaranteed results

No credible agency controls search engines, ad auctions, patient demand or the clinic's sales process. Look for clear hypotheses, testing logic, reporting and accountability rather than guaranteed rankings or guaranteed patient numbers.

Specialist knowledge matters, but so does integration

A clinic may hire separate suppliers for social, SEO, PPC, development and CRM. That can work, but someone must own the overall journey. Otherwise each supplier can report a successful channel while the clinic still struggles to convert enquiries.

Good agency selection question: “If you could only change three things in our current marketing during the first 90 days, what would they be and why?” The answer usually reveals whether the agency has thought commercially about your clinic.
