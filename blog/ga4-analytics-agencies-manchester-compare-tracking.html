Start with the business problem

Analytics setup should give the business a dependable measurement system, not simply another dashboard. Before choosing a Manchester GA4 agency, agree which actions matter and who proves the data is being collected correctly.

A tracking plan should start with the questions the business needs answered. Collecting every possible event can create noise, while missing the one meaningful form or booking action makes the implementation much less useful.

Ask who is responsible for implementation

Ask for an event specification before deployment. It should name the event, trigger, page or component, parameters and intended reporting use. Document account access and any GTM changes.

Do not assume a strategy, audit or setup line automatically includes every practical change needed to make it live. Put dependencies and internal responsibilities into the proposal.

Agree how success will be measured

After implementation, test real journeys rather than relying on configuration screenshots. Submit forms, click calls or complete bookings as appropriate and confirm the events appear sensibly.

Decide the reporting model before delivery so both sides know which numbers are useful and which are merely diagnostic.

Protect ownership and handover

Keep appropriate business ownership of platforms, data and core accounts. Document administrator access, exports and what happens if the supplier relationship ends.

Ask for proof that matches the work

Ask for a tracking plan, change log and post-launch QA evidence for the priority conversion journeys.

Where client information is confidential, a supplier can still demonstrate process quality through redacted documents, owned properties or implementation evidence without inventing outcomes.

Watch for this red flag

Red flag: The agency says tracking is complete because the GA4 tag appears on the site, but cannot show whether important conversion actions fire correctly.
