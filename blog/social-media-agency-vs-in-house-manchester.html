Social Media Agency vs In-House Manchester: Which Should Your Business Hire?

If your business is ready to invest in consistent social media, the choice may be between outsourcing and hiring someone internally. The right answer depends on how much daily access you need, how broad the creative workload is and whether social media is a full-time role or one part of a wider marketing system.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
Built for buyers, not beginners. This guide assumes you are already considering a supplier and focuses on scope, contracts, ownership, pricing structure and provider selection.

Choose in-house when constant access is the priority

An employee can live inside the business, capture day-to-day content quickly and build deep product knowledge. That is valuable when social media needs frequent on-site activity, rapid approvals or close coordination with internal teams.

Choose an agency when you need several skills without several hires

Social media can require strategy, copy, graphic design, short-form video, scheduling, reporting and paid-media knowledge. An agency can spread those skills across a team rather than expecting one employee to be equally strong at everything.

Use your content-capture needs as the deciding factor

If your content depends on daily filming inside a restaurant, factory or event venue, an internal creator can have an advantage. If most assets can be planned and supplied in batches, an agency may be more efficient.

Compare management time as well as supplier cost

An employee needs line management, development and workload planning. An agency still needs approvals and good briefing, but the production team manages its own internal delivery. Include your management time when comparing options.

A hybrid model can be sensible

Some businesses keep an internal marketing lead while outsourcing design, video, paid ads or campaign execution. If the workload is broad, the decision does not have to be entirely agency or entirely in-house.

Frequently asked questions

Is an agency cheaper than hiring a social media manager?

It can be, especially when you need several creative skills but not a full-time employee. Compare total employment and management costs with the agency scope.

When is an in-house social media hire better?

When frequent on-site content, immediate business access and day-to-day internal coordination are essential.

Can an agency understand my brand well enough?

Yes, but onboarding, approvals and regular communication matter. Ask how the agency collects brand information and feedback.

What does Kydos Starter include?

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, three posts per week, graphic design, video editing, content strategy/calendar and stories two to three times per week.

Can I use both an internal person and an agency?

Yes. A hybrid model can work well when the internal team owns brand access and the agency supplies specialist production or paid-media capability.

Which option is better for a small business?

If social media does not justify a full-time role, an agency package can provide broader skills at a more flexible cost. The final choice depends on content and access requirements.
