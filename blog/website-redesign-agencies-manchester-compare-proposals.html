Website Redesign Agencies Manchester: Compare Proposals Before You Rebuild

If you are already looking for a Manchester agency to redesign an existing website, the biggest risk is not choosing the wrong colour palette. It is paying for a rebuild that loses useful search visibility, breaks tracking, removes important content or recreates the same conversion problems in a prettier layout.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
Built for buyers, not beginners. This guide assumes you are already considering a supplier and focuses on scope, contracts, ownership, pricing structure and provider selection.

Make every redesign proposal answer the same business problem

Before comparing fees, write down why the current website is being replaced. Typical reasons include weak mobile experience, poor conversion, outdated design, difficult editing, slow performance, limited service architecture or disconnected forms and CRM. Ask each shortlisted agency to explain which of those problems its proposal is designed to solve.

Demand an SEO migration plan if the current site already ranks

A redesign can damage organic visibility when useful URLs disappear or page topics change without redirects. Ask whether the agency will review indexed pages, existing search traffic, backlinks and important landing pages before changing structure.

Buyer-stage red flag: the proposal recommends replacing every URL but does not mention redirects, existing rankings or Search Console data.

Ask what happens to analytics and lead tracking

A redesigned website should not go live with broken conversion tracking. Ask which forms, calls, bookings or purchases are currently measured, and how those events will be tested after launch. If paid media is running, tracking continuity is especially important.

Clarify what survives from the current website

Useful content, customer resources, legal pages, downloads, forms and integrations may already work well. A good redesign proposal should distinguish between what needs replacing and what should be preserved.

Choose the proposal with the clearest launch checklist

The strongest redesign proposal should explain mobile QA, browser checks, redirects, forms, analytics, metadata, indexing and post-launch support. A new design is only one part of a successful migration.

Frequently asked questions

Should I redesign or completely rebuild my website?

That depends on the platform, current architecture and the problems you need to fix. Ask agencies to explain which parts can be retained and which genuinely require rebuilding.

Can a website redesign hurt SEO?

Yes, especially if high-performing URLs, content or internal links are removed without a migration plan. SEO preservation should be discussed before development.

Should analytics be part of a redesign?

Yes. Existing measurement should be reviewed and key conversion events checked after launch so you do not lose visibility into performance.

Does Kydos redesign existing websites?

Yes. Kydos can rebuild or improve an existing website when its structure, mobile experience, conversion journey or integrations are holding the business back.

How should I compare redesign quotes?

Compare page scope, content, migration, SEO, forms, integrations, analytics, revisions, support and ownership, not only design style and total price.

What should I send an agency before requesting a redesign quote?

Share the current website, business goals, key pages, known problems, required integrations and the main action you want visitors to take.
