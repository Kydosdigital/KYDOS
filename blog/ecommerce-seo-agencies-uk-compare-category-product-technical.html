Start with the commercial outcome

Ecommerce SEO is not simply adding keywords to product descriptions. The provider has to manage category intent, product discoverability, technical crawl issues and the way large catalogues create duplicate or low-value URLs.

Large stores need prioritisation. The agency should focus first on categories and products with meaningful demand, margin and availability rather than trying to optimise the entire catalogue at once.

Make implementation responsibility explicit

Ask for a keyword-to-page map and a technical crawl plan. If the store has many filtered URLs, variants or discontinued products, the proposal should explain how those are handled.

List any work that depends on your developer, ecommerce team, sales team or another supplier. A good proposal should make those boundaries obvious before launch.

Agree the measurement model

Traffic growth should be interpreted alongside organic revenue, product availability and category performance. A ranking win on an out-of-stock or low-margin product may not matter commercially.

Decide which metrics are diagnostic and which ones determine whether the investment is commercially useful.

Protect account ownership and data

Keep appropriate business access to advertising, analytics, store and CRM accounts. Confirm what happens to campaigns, audiences, feeds, data and creative if the supplier changes.

Ask for proof that matches the service

Ask for a prioritised category roadmap and evidence of how technical recommendations will actually be implemented.

Where client data is confidential, the provider can still show redacted structures, workflow examples or implementation evidence without inventing results.

Watch for this red flag

Red flag: The agency promises thousands of new product descriptions but has not audited categories, faceted navigation, canonicals or indexation.

Frequently asked questions

How should I compare ecommerce SEO providers?

Compare scope, implementation, ownership, reporting, exclusions and the commercial outcome each proposal is expected to improve.

Should I choose the lowest fee?

Not automatically. Compare media, software, production, implementation and support costs that may sit outside the headline fee.

What should be agreed before launch?

Agree goals, responsibilities, access, tracking, approvals, budget boundaries and the conditions for scaling or changing direction.
