Electrician Marketing Agency UK: Provider Checklist

Choose a provider around the electrical work you want to win, not around a generic promise of more leads.

Updated 2 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guide

Separate the markets you serve

Domestic call-outs, rewires, inspection work, EV charging and commercial contracts can involve different buyers and economics. Ask the agency to show how campaigns, service pages and qualification will distinguish them.

Make local coverage operationally accurate

Define the radius your team can serve efficiently. For commercial work, the footprint may be wider. A good provider should align targeting with real capacity rather than chase rankings in every nearby town.

Use real credentials as trust evidence

Electrical buyers care about competence and safety. Supply accurate qualifications, memberships and project evidence, then ask how the agency will place them at the points where prospects make decisions.

A £100 enquiry and a multi-day project should not automatically be treated as equivalent conversions. Agree what useful demand looks like and how booked-job feedback can improve future targeting.

Frequently asked questions

Should domestic and commercial electrical marketing be separate?

Often yes because buyers, job values, sales cycles and proof requirements can differ.

Should EV charger work have its own campaign?

If it is a priority service with distinct demand, it may warrant separate targeting and landing content.

Who verifies credentials?

Your business should supply and approve accurate qualifications, memberships and certifications.

Should calls and forms both be tracked?

Where both are meaningful enquiry routes, measuring both gives a more complete view.

Who owns the website content?

Check the contract for ownership, licences and access before signing.

What is the best agency KPI?

Useful enquiries and downstream outcomes are stronger commercial measures than traffic alone.
