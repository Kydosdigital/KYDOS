WordPress Agencies Manchester: Compare Builds, Ownership & Support

If you are already comparing Manchester WordPress agencies, the platform name tells you very little about the quality of the finished site. The buying decision should focus on how the site is built, who owns the accounts, which recurring costs exist and who is responsible when themes or plugins need attention.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
Built for buyers, not beginners. This guide assumes you are already considering a supplier and focuses on scope, contracts, ownership, pricing structure and provider selection.

Ask whether the build is genuinely custom or mainly a theme setup

There is nothing automatically wrong with using a quality theme, but the proposal should be clear. Ask whether the design is custom, heavily adapted or based mostly on a prebuilt layout. That affects both price and how easy the site is to differentiate later.

List every recurring plugin and hosting cost

WordPress sites can depend on paid plugins for forms, backups, security, ecommerce, page building or SEO functionality. Ask the agency to list recurring licences and whether those accounts belong to you or the agency.

Check how the agency handles performance and plugin bloat

A WordPress build can become slow when too many plugins and scripts are added without discipline. Ask how the agency approaches image optimisation, caching, unnecessary scripts and ongoing updates rather than assuming the platform itself guarantees performance.

Ask whether SEO architecture is planned before the build

If organic search matters, the site should allow dedicated service, location and supporting pages without forcing everything into one generic page. Basic titles and an SEO plugin are not the same as a search-led architecture.

Clarify maintenance after launch

WordPress requires updates to the core software, themes and plugins. Ask whether maintenance is included, optional or your responsibility, and what happens if an update causes a conflict.

Choose a provider based on the website's job, not WordPress loyalty

The right agency should be willing to explain why WordPress suits your requirements rather than recommending it automatically. The platform is a tool. Your conversion, editing, SEO and integration needs should determine the final setup.

Frequently asked questions

Should I only hire a specialist WordPress agency?

If you specifically need WordPress, specialist experience can help. Still compare design quality, support, ownership, SEO and integration capability.

Do WordPress websites have monthly costs?

They can. Hosting, premium plugins, backups, security and maintenance may create recurring costs, so ask for a first-year ownership estimate.

Who should own the WordPress hosting account?

Your business should have clear access and transfer rights. Clarify ownership before the project begins.

Is WordPress good for SEO?

It can support strong SEO, but rankings depend on site structure, content, technical quality and authority, not the CMS alone.

Does Kydos only build WordPress websites?

Kydos web services are scoped around the project's needs. The public website offering also includes ecommerce options through WooCommerce or Shopify where required.

What should I compare between WordPress proposals?

Compare theme approach, page scope, plugins, hosting, licences, content, SEO, performance, maintenance, support and ownership.
