Supplier-selection guide: this starts from the point where you are already considering outside help for Shopify marketing.

Start with the commercial outcome

A Shopify growth agency may offer paid ads, email, SEO and creative under one retainer. The key buying question is whether those channels are coordinated around margin, repeat purchase and profitable acquisition rather than reported separately.

Revenue alone can hide unprofitable growth. The provider should understand average order value, gross margin, return rate, repeat purchase and the acceptable cost to acquire a customer.

Make implementation responsibility explicit

Ask the agency to identify the first bottleneck before spreading budget across every channel. A store with weak conversion may need landing or product-page work before more ad spend.

List any work that depends on your developer, ecommerce team, sales team or another supplier. A good proposal should make those boundaries obvious before launch.

Agree the measurement model

Useful reporting should connect spend to orders, revenue and customer quality while acknowledging attribution limits. No single ad platform should be treated as perfect truth.

Decide which metrics are diagnostic and which ones determine whether the investment is commercially useful.

Protect account ownership and data

Keep appropriate business access to advertising, analytics, store and CRM accounts. Confirm what happens to campaigns, audiences, feeds, data and creative if the supplier changes.

Ask for proof that matches the service

Ask for a first-90-day growth plan that names the starting channel, assumptions, measurement and scale criteria.

Where client data is confidential, the provider can still show redacted structures, workflow examples or implementation evidence without inventing results.

Watch for this red flag

Red flag: The agency proposes scaling ad spend immediately without asking about margin, fulfilment capacity, repeat purchase or current conversion rate.

Frequently asked questions

How should I compare Shopify marketing providers?

Compare scope, implementation, ownership, reporting, exclusions and the commercial outcome each proposal is expected to improve.

Should I choose the lowest fee?

Not automatically. Compare media, software, production, implementation and support costs that may sit outside the headline fee.

What should be agreed before launch?

Agree goals, responsibilities, access, tracking, approvals, budget boundaries and the conditions for scaling or changing direction.

What is a warning sign?

The agency proposes scaling ad spend immediately without asking about margin, fulfilment capacity, repeat purchase or current conversion rate.
