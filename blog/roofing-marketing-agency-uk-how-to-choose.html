Roofing Marketing Agency UK: Hiring Checklist

Roofing demand can range from urgent leak repairs to planned replacements and commercial contracts. Your agency shortlist should reflect those different economics.

Updated 2 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guide

Separate urgent and planned demand

A homeowner with active water ingress behaves differently from someone comparing a full reroof. Ask agencies how keyword targeting, landing pages, call handling and budgets will distinguish those journeys instead of treating every roofing enquiry alike.

Make geography commercially realistic

Define the areas you can serve profitably, especially for lower-value repair work. The provider should explain how local SEO and paid campaigns will respect that footprint without manufacturing thin location pages.

Ask how proof gets produced

Roof condition, workmanship and finished results are visual. Agree how site photos, project summaries, guarantees or relevant credentials supplied by your business will be organised and published. Do not accept invented proof.

Frequently asked questions

Should roofing campaigns separate repairs from replacements?

Usually yes when intent, urgency, value and qualification differ materially.

Should weather affect campaign planning?

Demand can change with weather and season, so ask how budgets and messaging will be reviewed rather than left static.

Should calls be tracked?

Call tracking can help assess campaign enquiries when implemented appropriately alongside forms and other outcomes.

How important are reviews?

Reviews can support local trust, but they should sit alongside genuine project evidence and clear service information.

Who owns Google Ads accounts?

Your business should have clear access and understand ownership before the engagement starts.

What should reporting show?

It should show useful enquiries by source where possible, not only impressions and clicks.
