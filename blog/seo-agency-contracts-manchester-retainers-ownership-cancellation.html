SEO Agency Contract Checklist · Manchester

SEO Agency Contracts Manchester: Retainers, Ownership & Cancellation Terms

SEO contracts can look simple because the service is monthly, but the important details sit in the scope and exit terms. If you are already choosing a Manchester SEO agency, check what you actually receive each month, who owns the work and how easily you can leave if the service is not a fit.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
For buyers already comparing suppliers. This guide focuses on provider selection, scope, ownership, pricing structure and commercial terms rather than beginner-level marketing education.

Check the minimum commitment and notice period

SEO often needs sustained work, but that does not mean every long contract is justified. Ask why the proposed term is necessary, what should be delivered within it and how much notice is required to cancel.

Make recurring deliverables explicit

The contract should explain whether keyword research, on-page work, technical fixes, content, links, local SEO and reporting are included and at what level.

Protect ownership of content and accounts

Clarify ownership of written content, Search Console, Google Analytics, Google Business Profile and any SEO tools or dashboards that matter to your business.

Separate recommendations from implementation

ReportingHow often and against which KPIs?

Review what happens on cancellation

Ask whether created content remains on your site, whether access is removed, how outstanding work is handled and whether there are final reporting or handover obligations.

Check whether performance promises are contractual

Be cautious with guaranteed ranking clauses or vague promises of “page one”. No agency controls Google's results. A better agreement defines work and reporting rather than impossible guarantees.

Sign the contract that matches the actual SEO plan

If the proposal is heavily technical, the contract should include implementation. If content is central, the writing and approval process should be defined. Generic contracts often signal generic delivery.

Frequently asked questions

How long should an SEO contract be?

There is no universal term. Ask what the agency expects to accomplish within the proposed period and review notice/cancellation rights.

Who owns SEO content created by an agency?

The contract should say. Clarify ownership and use rights before work begins.

Should Search Console access belong to the business?

Your business should retain appropriate access to core analytics and search accounts.

Can an SEO agency guarantee rankings?

No credible agency can control Google rankings. Treat hard ranking guarantees cautiously.
