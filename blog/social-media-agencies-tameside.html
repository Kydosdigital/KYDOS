Start with the commercial objective

Tameside businesses often serve a practical local catchment that overlaps with east Manchester, Stockport and nearby boroughs. A sensible local strategy should follow that actual customer footprint rather than assuming every town within Tameside needs its own separate page or campaign.

Before requesting proposals, decide which enquiries, services or customer segments matter most. That gives every agency the same brief and makes generic packages easier to challenge.

Make Tameside earn its place in the plan

A business focused on Ashton-under-Lyne, Hyde, Denton, Stalybridge, Droylsden and surrounding parts of the borough may need a different structure from one serving all of Greater Manchester. The provider should explain why each local page, campaign or targeting choice exists.

Challenge the service-specific assumption

Local social content should reflect real customers, events, projects or service activity rather than simply repeating a Tameside hashtag.

Ask what the agency will actually implement

Recommendations are not execution. Clarify what the agency changes directly, what depends on your team, and what needs another supplier. This matters when the work touches the website, analytics, advertising accounts or CRM.

Protect ownership and access

Your business should understand who owns the website, ad accounts, analytics properties, social profiles, creative files, CRM data and third-party subscriptions. Agree what happens to access if you change supplier.

Demand useful reporting

Reporting should connect activity to the action the business values, such as qualified calls, quote requests, consultations, bookings or sales. Local visibility is useful only when it supports a customer journey.

Check the local-content workflow before agreeing the calendar

If the strategy depends on real activity across Ashton-under-Lyne, Hyde, Denton, Stalybridge and Droylsden, clarify who captures photos, short-form video, customer proof and team updates. Then ask how often local context is genuinely useful compared with broader service, brand and educational content. This protects the business from signing a high-volume package that looks attractive on paper but depends on a stream of raw material nobody has agreed to produce.

What should the first 90 days show?

The provider should state what it will audit, what it will change first, which commercial pages or campaigns receive priority, how tracking will be checked and what evidence would justify expanding the scope.
