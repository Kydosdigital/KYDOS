Ecommerce Web Design Agencies Manchester: Compare Shopify, WooCommerce & Scope

An ecommerce website quote can look simple until product migration, payment setup, shipping rules, subscriptions, analytics and custom checkout requirements are added. If you are already choosing a Manchester ecommerce agency, compare the operating model of the store, not just the homepage design.

Updated 1 September 2026Decision-stage buyer guideManchester
Built for buyers, not beginners. This guide assumes you are already considering a supplier and focuses on scope, contracts, ownership, pricing structure and provider selection.

Choose the platform around your operating needs

Shopify and WooCommerce can both support serious ecommerce businesses, but the better fit depends on products, internal editing, customisation, integrations, subscriptions and maintenance preferences. Ask the agency why it recommends a platform for your store rather than accepting a default choice.

Make product and migration scope explicit

Clarify how many products are being added or migrated, who supplies descriptions and images, how variants are handled and whether customer/order history needs moving from an existing system.

Ask who owns the platform and payment accounts

The business should understand who owns the Shopify account, hosting, payment gateway, analytics and any third-party apps. Do not wait until handover to discover that key subscriptions sit under an agency-controlled account.

Compare support after the first sale

An ecommerce site is operational software. Ask how bugs, checkout issues, app conflicts and urgent updates are handled after launch. The post-launch support model matters more here than on a simple brochure website.

Choose the agency that understands the buying journey

Product discovery, category navigation, product detail pages, cart, checkout and post-purchase communication all affect conversion. A strong ecommerce proposal should explain how those stages are handled, not only how the homepage will look.

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose Shopify or WooCommerce?

The better choice depends on your editing needs, customisation, integrations, maintenance preference and existing systems. Ask the agency to justify the recommendation.

Does Kydos build ecommerce stores?

Yes. Kydos offers ecommerce development through WooCommerce or Shopify with Basic and Advanced scopes.

Is product upload normally included?

Do not assume so. Confirm the number of products, who supplies data and whether migration from an existing store is included.

Should ecommerce SEO be included?

Basic SEO setup can be part of the build, but ongoing ecommerce SEO is a separate programme. Clarify category, product and technical work.

What should I ask about payment gateways?

Confirm which providers are supported, who owns the accounts, how test transactions are handled and whether fees are separate.

How should I compare ecommerce agency quotes?

Compare product scope, platform, checkout, payments, shipping, integrations, analytics, SEO, migration, ownership and post-launch support.
