Questions

What businesses usually want to know before handing over their social channels.

Clear scope matters. If your requirements sit outside these packages, we can tell you that before work starts rather than hiding it inside vague wording.

What does social media management include?
Depending on your package, we can handle content planning, captions, graphic design, short-form video editing, scheduling, publishing, community management and performance reporting.
Which platforms can Kydos manage?
The core published plans cover Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Additional platform support can be scoped when your business also needs LinkedIn, YouTube or another relevant channel.
Can I review content before it is published?
Yes. A review and approval step can be built into the workflow before content is published.
Do I need to provide photos and videos?
Existing brand photography and video make the work more specific to your business. We can work with what you already have and identify where additional material would strengthen the content.
Is paid advertising included?
Paid ads management is included in Pipeline and Partner. Advertising spend is separate and is paid by the client.
Is there a long-term contract?
The published social media offer is designed without a long-term lock-in contract. The package scope and billing terms are confirmed before work starts.
Do you work outside Manchester?
Yes. Kydos is based in Manchester and works with businesses across the UK. Planning, approvals, meetings and reporting can all be handled remotely.
What happens after I enquire?
We review your current social presence, business and goals, then recommend the smallest sensible package. If you proceed, we move into onboarding, content planning and production.

Want us to take social media off your weekly to-do list?

Tell us what you are currently doing, what is not working and what you want social media to contribute to the business.
