Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Choose a Social Media Manager

The practical questions we would want answered before paying an agency every month.

What does social media management include?
Depending on your package, Kydos Digital can handle content planning, captions, graphic design, short-form video editing, scheduling, publishing, community management and performance reporting. The package cards above show the exact scope at each level.
Which social media platforms can Kydos manage?
Our core plans cover major platforms including Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. Additional platform support is available for businesses that also need LinkedIn, YouTube or another relevant channel.
Can I review content before it is published?
Yes. Your content is created around your brand, offers and audience, and a review and approval step can be included in the workflow before publishing.
Do I need to provide photos and videos?
Existing brand photography and video are useful because they make your content more specific to your business. We can work with the assets you already have and advise which additional photos or clips would strengthen future content.
Is paid advertising included?
Paid ads management is included in the Pipeline and Partner plans. Advertising spend itself is paid separately by the client, so your media budget remains distinct from the management fee.
Is there a long-term contract?
Our published social media offer is designed without a long-term lock-in contract. The package scope, billing terms and any specific project requirements are confirmed before work starts.
Do you work with businesses outside Manchester?
Yes. Kydos Digital is based in Manchester and works with businesses across the UK. Meetings, approvals, content planning and reporting can all be handled remotely.
What happens after I enquire?
We review your business, current social presence and goals, then recommend the smallest sensible package for what you need. If you proceed, we collect your brand information and move into onboarding and content planning.
