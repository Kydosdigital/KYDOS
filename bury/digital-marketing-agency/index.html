The short answer

What should a digital marketing agency do for a Bury business?

It should identify the highest-value commercial problem, choose the smallest useful channel mix, make implementation responsibilities clear and measure whether marketing is creating the action the business actually needs. A full-service agency is most useful when the bottleneck crosses channels; it should not become an excuse to sell every service at once.

Kydos in one sentence

Full-funnel diagnosis

More traffic is not always the next fix.

Before adding budget or publishing more content, identify where the current journey loses value. The marketing plan should change depending on the bottleneck.

Not enough qualified demand

Prioritise the channels that can reach or capture the right audience, such as SEO, PPC or consistent organic social content.

Traffic but weak conversion

Improve the offer, landing page, mobile UX, proof, forms and calls to action before paying to send more people into the same problem.

Leads but poor follow-up

Use clearer CRM ownership, pipeline stages and automation so response speed and nurture do not depend on somebody remembering manually.

Agency vs separate suppliers

When does a full-service agency make sense?

A broader agency is useful when channels need to share one commercial plan. Separate specialists can still be the better choice when the requirement is narrow, highly technical or already coordinated by a strong internal team.

Which digital marketing services can Kydos combine?

Kydos can support social media management, SEO, PPC and paid advertising, websites and landing pages, CRM and marketing automation, and analytics or conversion tracking. The proposal should include only the services needed for the current commercial problem.

Can I hire Kydos for only one service?

Yes. A business does not need to buy a full-service retainer. Kydos can scope a focused service such as social media, SEO, PPC or web development when that is the clearest priority.

Is advertising spend included in the agency fee?

No. Media spend for platforms such as Google, Meta or LinkedIn is separate from agency management unless a proposal explicitly states otherwise.

Can you target Bury and wider Greater Manchester without duplicating pages?

Yes. The site and campaign architecture should assign one strongest page or campaign to each distinct intent, then use local support only where the buyer need, service area or search demand is genuinely different.

What happens after a lead is generated?

Should I start with SEO or PPC?

PPC can capture high-intent demand and generate testing data quickly, while SEO builds longer-term organic visibility. The right starting point depends on search demand, budget, website quality and how quickly the business needs learning or enquiries.

Can Kydos build or improve the landing page too?

Yes. Website and landing-page work can be scoped when the destination page is limiting conversion, tracking or campaign performance.
