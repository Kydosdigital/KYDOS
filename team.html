The operating model

One brief should not become five disconnected projects.

A campaign may need content, a landing page, conversion tracking and CRM follow-up. A website may need SEO, analytics and automation. The delivery model is designed so those dependencies stay visible.

Lead

One accountable strategic/client lead keeps the objective and decisions clear.

Plan

The required specialists are brought in according to the problem, not according to a pre-set package.

Deliver

Creative and technical work is coordinated against the same scope and timeline.

Review

Performance, sales feedback and implementation issues feed into the next iteration.

Working principle

The client should not have to manage the gaps between strategy, creative and implementation. That coordination is part of the agency's job.
