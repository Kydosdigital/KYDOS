About the Role

What you'll be doing

We're looking for a Digital Marketing Manager to join our growing digital marketing agency in Manchester. You'll lead digital marketing campaigns, develop strategies, manage client accounts, and drive measurable results for our clients.

This is a hands-on, client-facing role with real ownership. You'll work across multiple accounts, collaborate with our developers and designers, and have the autonomy to shape how campaigns are built and run.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan, execute, and optimise digital marketing campaigns across Google Ads, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and other platforms
  • Develop and implement digital marketing strategies aligned with client business objectives
  • Manage marketing budgets and track ROI across all campaigns
  • Conduct market research, competitor analysis, and identify growth opportunities
  • Create content calendars and oversee content creation (blogs, social media, emails)
  • Monitor analytics, generate reports, and present findings to clients and internal teams
  • Manage relationships with key clients and act as their primary marketing contact
  • Lead and mentor junior team members (if applicable)
  • Stay current with digital marketing trends, platform updates, and best practices
  • Collaborate with developers, designers, and other teams to deliver integrated marketing solutions

The Successful Applicant

  • Strong background in marketing with relevant qualifications
  • Proven expertise in Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram), and analytics platforms
  • Strong understanding of SEO, content marketing, and email marketing
  • Excellent analytical and data-driven decision-making skills
  • Project management and organisational skills
  • Proficiency in marketing software (HubSpot, Google Analytics, or similar)
